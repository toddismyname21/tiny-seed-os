## 1. PURPOSE

This letter serves to document the existing operational agreement between Landlord and Tenant regarding the agricultural use of land, buildings, and equipment for organic produce production. This agreement formalizes the verbal understanding that has been in effect and satisfies the requirements of:
- Agricultural lending institutions
- USDA Farm Service Agency (FSA)
- Pennsylvania Beginning Farmer Tax Credit Program (Act 65 of 2019)

---

## 2. TERM

This agreement is effective from **January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026**.

It is the intent of both parties to renew this agreement annually. Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice.

---

## 5. CAPITAL REPAIRS VS. MAINTENANCE

### 5.1 Landlord Responsibility (Capital Repairs)
- Replacement of major equipment components
- Structural repairs to buildings
- Greenhouse covering replacement (4-year cycle)
- Refrigeration system repairs (compressor, thermostat, coolant)
- Major tractor repairs beyond normal wear

### 5.2 Tenant Responsibility (Routine Maintenance)
- Cleaning and sanitizing coolers
- Lubrication and inspection of equipment
- Oil changes and filter replacement on motorized equipment
- Winterizing plumbing and irrigation
- Maintaining mowed roadways and areas around buildings

### 5.3 Shared/Negotiated
- Items not clearly falling into either category to be discussed before work is performed
- Emergency repairs: Tenant may proceed and seek reimbursement for capital items

## 6. ORGANIC CERTIFICATION

The property is USDA Certified Organic. The Tenant agrees to maintain all farming practices in strict accordance with National Organic Program (NOP) standards. Any activity that would jeopardize organic certification is prohibited.

---

## 7. INSURANCE

Tenant agrees to maintain liability insurance coverage and provide evidence of such to Landlord upon request.

## 8. PENNSYLVANIA BEGINNING FARMER TAX CREDIT

This written lease agreement satisfies the requirements of Pennsylvania's Beginning Farmer Tax Credit Program (Act 65 of 2019). The Landlord may be eligible for a state income tax credit equal to 10% of gross rental income (up to $7,000/year) for three years by renting to a certified Beginning Farmer.

**Estimated Landlord Tax Credit:** ~$2,100/year × 3 years = **~$6,300 total**

Tenant agrees to cooperate with any documentation required for the Landlord to claim this credit.
