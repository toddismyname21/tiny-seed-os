## A3. "Double Acreage" Clause - Non-Standard and Excessive

Current arrangement charges tenant based on "double the land annually cropped":

| Actual Acres Farmed | Charged For | Rate | Effective Cost |
|---------------------|-------------|------|----------------|
| 1.5 acres | 3 acres | $500/acre | **$1,000 per acre actually farmed** |

**Market comparison:**
- Western PA agricultural land: $70-110/acre
- Current effective rate: $1,000/acre (10-14× market)

This clause is not industry standard. Most farm leases charge for actual land used, with access roads/boundaries either included in the rate or charged at a reduced rate.

**REQUESTED RESOLUTION:** Eliminate "double acreage" clause. Charge for actual acres at $150/acre (still above market, reflecting infrastructure premium).

### ARTICLE 8: CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS & MAINTENANCE

**This is the critical section. Clear definitions prevent future disputes.**

#### 8.1 LANDLORD RESPONSIBILITY (Capital Repairs)

The **LESSOR** is responsible for all capital costs, defined as repairs which:
- Result in a betterment to the property
- Restore the property to working condition
- Adapt property to a new or different use

**Specifically, LESSOR pays for:**
- Refrigeration equipment repairs (compressors, thermostats, charging)
- Electrical system repairs
- Heating system repairs (fans, burners, thermostats)
- Structural repairs to buildings
- Plumbing repairs (beyond winterizing)
- Major equipment repairs (engine, transmission, hydraulics)
- Tree removal, grading, or site preparation
- Greenhouse frame repairs

#### 8.2 TENANT RESPONSIBILITY (Maintenance)

The **LESSEE** is responsible for routine maintenance, defined as:
- Cleaning and sanitizing (coolers, wash areas)
- Lubrication and inspection
- Changing oil and filters on motorized equipment
- Winterizing plumbing and irrigation
- Replacing greenhouse plastic (see 8.3)
- Minor adjustments and calibration
- Keeping equipment clean and properly stored

#### 8.3 GREENHOUSE PLASTIC (Shared Cost)

Greenhouse plastic is a grey area with short useful life (3-4 years).

**AGREED COST SHARING:**
- **LESSOR:** 50% of materials cost
- **LESSEE:** 50% of materials cost + 100% of labor

*Current estimated cost: $2,200 for 3 greenhouses = $1,100 each party for materials*

#### 8.4 WEAR ITEMS INCLUDED IN EQUIPMENT RENTAL

The following are considered normal wear and are **INCLUDED** in equipment rental fees:
- Hoses (water, hydraulic)
- Belts and filters
- Windshield wipers (vehicles)
- Light bulbs
- Small hardware (nuts, bolts, clamps)

#### 8.5 REPAIR AUTHORIZATION

Repairs exceeding $200 require written approval from Lessor before work begins, except in emergencies threatening crop loss or safety.
