### The "Double Acreage" Clause

**From the Kretschmann Lease Template:**
> "Acreage will be calculated as double the land annually cropped in the year of the lease."

**Industry Standard:** This is **NOT standard practice**. Most agricultural leases charge for:
- Actual acres farmed, OR

- Higher soil quality requirements
- Irrigation infrastructure needs
- Proximity to markets

# SECTION 2: CAPITAL vs. MAINTENANCE REPAIRS

## The Industry Standard (Farm Commons)

> "Capital costs for repair to buildings and equipment are borne by the lessor [landlord]. Maintenance costs by the lessee [tenant]."

### Defining the Difference

| Type | Definition | Who Pays |
|------|------------|----------|
| **Capital Repair** | Restores property to working condition, materially enhances value, or extends useful life | **LANDLORD** |
| **Maintenance** | Routine upkeep, cleaning, lubricating, minor adjustments | **TENANT** |

### Specific Examples from Farm Commons

**LANDLORD Pays For:**
- Water supply systems (well pump, irrigation)
- Structural components (siding, roofing, fences)
- Waste treatment systems
- **Heating and ventilating systems**
- Major structural repairs
- **Refrigeration equipment repairs**
- Electrical system repairs

**TENANT Pays For:**
- Cleaning and sanitizing
- Lubrication and inspection
- **Changing oil on motorized equipment** ✓
- Winterizing plumbing
- Minor adjustments

# SECTION 5: THE "TRIPLE NET" PROBLEM

## What is Triple Net (NNN)?

A lease structure where tenant pays:
- Base rent, PLUS
- Property taxes
- Insurance
- ALL maintenance and repairs

## Is NNN Standard for Farmland?

**NO.** Research from LoopNet and agricultural law sources confirms:

> "NNN leases are designed for commercial real estate... A landlord proposing NNN for farmland may be trying to extract commercial-level returns from agricultural land."

**Red flag:**
> "If a landlord proposes an 'absolute triple net' or NNN lease for farmland, this shifts even capital improvements (roof, structure, wells) to the tenant - this is exploitative for working farms."

### Your Situation

Don's spreadsheet model functions like a triple net lease:
- You pay base rent
- You pay for capital repairs (cooler, heater, trees)
- You pay for wear items (hoses, wipers)
- You pay for infrastructure (cold frame charges)

This is **not standard for working farmland.**
