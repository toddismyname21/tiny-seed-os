PART 2: Labeling Review

Review the OEFFA Labeling Fact Sheet (attached PDF). Check off each item once you understand it.

  • Decide your labeling category for each product you sell:
    • 100% Organic — every ingredient is certified organic
    • Organic (95%+) — remaining 5% from NOP allowed list
    • Made With Organic [X] (70%+) — name up to 3 organic ingredients
  • Check all current labels for required elements:
    • "Certified Organic By OEFFA" directly below your farm name/address
    • Each organic ingredient marked with "Organic" or asterisk (*)
    • Net weight on package
  • Wholesale/bulk containers must have:
    • Product identified as organic
    • Lot number or shipping ID for audit trail
  • USDA Organic seal — optional for "100% Organic" and "Organic" categories. NOT allowed for "Made With Organic" category.
