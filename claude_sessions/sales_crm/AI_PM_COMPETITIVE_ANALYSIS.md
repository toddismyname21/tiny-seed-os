# AI PM App Competitive Analysis
## Product: Council of Wizards
## Date: 2026-01-29
## Analyst: Sales/CRM Claude

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Key Finding 1:** No competitor has true multi-agent AI architecture - all use single AI assistants that lack coordination and specialized roles.

**Key Finding 2:** Cross-session memory is the industry's biggest gap - competitors lose context between sessions, forcing users to re-explain their projects repeatedly.

**Key Finding 3:** Pricing ranges from free to $35/user/month, with AI typically an add-on cost ($7-$10/user/month extra) - opportunity for "AI included" positioning.

**Recommended Positioning:** "The AI team that manages your projects - not just another AI assistant."

**Confidence Level:** High (based on verified pricing pages, user reviews from G2/Capterra, and product documentation)

## MARKET GAPS (Where We Win)

### 1. No True Multi-Agent Architecture
Every competitor uses a single AI assistant. None have specialized agents that coordinate, learn different aspects, or check each other's work. Council's Overseer + Scribe + Artificer + Mentor model is unique.

### 2. Memory Is Session-Based
Competitors forget context between sessions. Users must re-explain their project, preferences, and history every time. Council's Scribe provides persistent memory across ALL interactions.

### 3. No Quality Verification Loop
AI outputs go directly to users without review. Council's Mentor agent reviews all work before delivery, catching errors and ensuring quality.

### 4. Reactive, Not Proactive
Competitors wait for users to ask. Council anticipates needs based on learned patterns and proactively surfaces insights, risks, and recommendations.

### 5. AI Is Expensive Add-On
Most competitors charge extra for AI ($7-10/user/month), or limit usage with credits. Council includes full AI with no limits.

## TOP 3 THREATS

### 1. **Notion AI Agents (2025)**
Why dangerous: Notion has 30M+ users and just launched autonomous AI Agents that work for 20 minutes across hundreds of pages. Their market presence and brand trust are massive.
**Counter:** Their agents are task-specific, not coordinated. No persistent memory or quality loop.

### 2. **Monday Agents (2026)**
Why dangerous: Monday.com has $1B+ ARR, public company resources, and announced purpose-built AI agents coming 2026. They'll market heavily.
**Counter:** We're first to market with multi-agent. Their agents are specialized (SDR agent) not general PM.

### 3. **ClickUp's Multi-Model Approach**
Why dangerous: ClickUp Brain lets users switch between GPT-5, Claude, o3 based on task. 8M+ users already on platform.
**Counter:** Multiple models isn't multi-agent. They have no coordination, memory, or quality loop.

---

## TOP 3 OPPORTUNITIES

### 1. **"AI Team" Positioning**
Why we win: No one else offers a "team of AI specialists." We can own the "AI that works like a team" narrative while others have "AI assistant."

### 2. **No-Limits Pricing**
Why we win: Credit systems and add-on fees frustrate users. "AI included, no limits" is a clear differentiator that simplifies purchasing decisions.

### 3. **Enterprise Memory Problem**
Why we win: Large teams lose institutional knowledge when people leave. Council's Scribe preserves project history and patterns. Position as "never lose project context again."
