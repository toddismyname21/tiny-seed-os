Mt. Lebanon CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Mt. Lebanon?
Yes! We deliver throughout Mt. Lebanon including all residential areas from Bower Hill to Sunset Hills to areas near Washington Road. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
Can I add flowers to my CSA delivery?
Absolutely! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers weekly flower bouquets that can be added to your vegetable CSA. Many Mt. Lebanon members love having fresh, locally-grown flowers delivered with their produce - perfect for entertaining or simply enjoying at home.
What makes your produce different?
Our vegetables are harvested within 24 hours of delivery. Unlike grocery store produce that's been in transit for days or weeks, our vegetables arrive at peak freshness and nutrition. Mt. Lebanon families notice the difference immediately.
Can I skip weeks when we're traveling?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly - perfect for Mt. Lebanon families with busy schedules and travel plans.
