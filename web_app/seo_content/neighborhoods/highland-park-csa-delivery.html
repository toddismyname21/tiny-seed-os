Serving the Highland Park Community

Highland Park is one of Pittsburgh's most beloved neighborhoods, known for its stunning green spaces, the Pittsburgh Zoo & PPG Aquarium, and the beautiful Highland Park Reservoir. Pick up your CSA at Bryant St. Market on your way home from a morning jog on the bike trails or after a family day at the zoo.

Highland Park CSA Pickup FAQ

Where do I pick up my CSA in Highland Park?
CSA pickup for Highland Park is at Bryant St. Market during regular market hours. It's a convenient location for all Highland Park residents, whether you live near the zoo, reservoir, or anywhere in the neighborhood.
What are the pickup hours at Bryant St. Market?
Your CSA box will be available during Bryant St. Market's regular operating hours. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 for specific times.
Can I add flowers to my CSA?
Absolutely! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers weekly flower bouquets that can be added to your vegetable CSA. Pick up your flowers with your produce at Bryant St. Market.
Can I skip weeks when I'm traveling?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly.
