Wexford represents the best of suburban North Hills living - growing families, excellent schools in the Pine-Richland district, and residents who value quality. That's exactly who our CSA is designed for.

Whether you're in an established neighborhood off Route 19, a newer development near the shops at Wexford, or anywhere in the surrounding Pine-Richland area, we bring the farm directly to your doorstep. No need to battle Route 19 traffic to find fresh, local produce.

Wexford families tell us they love the convenience of having truly local vegetables delivered weekly. Our farm in Rochester is just a short drive north, making us your closest connection to real local agriculture. Many Wexford members have visited us for pick-your-own flower days - it's a great family outing!

Wexford CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Wexford?
Yes! We deliver throughout Wexford and the Pine-Richland area, including neighborhoods off Route 19, newer developments, and established communities. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
What day do you deliver to Wexford?
Wexford deliveries happen every Wednesday between 10am and 6pm as part of our North Hills route. You'll receive a notification when your box is delivered.
Can you deliver flowers to Wexford?
Absolutely! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers flower subscriptions and individual bouquet deliveries to Wexford. Add flowers to your CSA or order them separately - perfect for brightening your home or special occasions.
Can I skip weeks for family vacations?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Perfect for summer vacation schedules. Your membership extends accordingly.
Do you offer organic vegetables?
We use organic growing practices and never use synthetic pesticides or herbicides. Our vegetables are grown naturally with care for the land and your family's health.
