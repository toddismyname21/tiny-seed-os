Serving the Zelienople Community

Zelienople embodies the small-town charm that makes Butler County special. With its historic Main Street, community events, and tight-knit neighborhoods, it's the kind of place where people appreciate knowing where their food comes from - and who grows it.

That's what makes Tiny Seed Farm a perfect fit for Zelienople. We're a local family farm located nearby in Rochester, PA, and we believe in the same values that make Zelienople great: community, quality, and doing things the right way.

When you join our CSA, you're not just getting vegetables - you're supporting local agriculture and connecting with the land that surrounds Butler County. Each week, you'll receive produce that was in our fields just hours before it reaches your Zelienople doorstep.

Zelienople CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Zelienople?
Yes! We deliver throughout Zelienople and the surrounding Butler County area. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
How far is your farm from Zelienople?
Our farm is located in Rochester, PA - very close to Zelienople. This proximity means your produce travels a short distance, ensuring maximum freshness. Butler County is one of our priority delivery areas!
What makes your produce different from the grocery store?
Our vegetables are harvested within 24 hours of delivery, not days or weeks like grocery store produce. You'll taste the difference immediately - crisper, more flavorful, and more nutritious because it hasn't been sitting in warehouses or trucks.
Can I skip weeks if needed?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly.
