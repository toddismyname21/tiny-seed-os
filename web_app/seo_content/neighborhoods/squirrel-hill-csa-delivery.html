Squirrel Hill CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Squirrel Hill?
Yes! We deliver throughout Squirrel Hill including all residential areas from Murray Avenue to Frick Park. If you're unsure whether your specific address is covered, contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177.
What day do you deliver to Squirrel Hill?
Squirrel Hill deliveries happen every Thursday. Boxes typically arrive between 10am and 6pm. You'll receive a text notification when your delivery is on its way and when it's been dropped off.
Is there an extra delivery fee for Squirrel Hill?
No! Delivery to Squirrel Hill is included in your membership price. There are no hidden fees or delivery charges.
Where do you leave the CSA box?
We deliver to your doorstep, porch, or a location you specify. For apartments, we can deliver to building entrances or specific instructions you provide. All boxes are insulated to keep produce fresh.
Can I skip a week if I'm traveling?
Absolutely! Just let us know in advance (by the weekend before your delivery) and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends by that week, so you never lose value.

About Tiny Seed Farm

Tiny Seed Farm is a family-owned farm in Rochester, PA, just north of Pittsburgh. We grow vegetables for Pittsburgh families, building a community of members who value fresh, local food and genuine relationships with their farmer.

Unlike aggregator platforms that act as middlemen between you and anonymous farms, when you join Tiny Seed Farm's CSA, you're buying directly from us. You can visit our farm, call Todd with questions, and know exactly where every vegetable in your box was grown.

We deliver throughout the Pittsburgh metro area, including Squirrel Hill, bringing farm-fresh produce directly to your door. No pickup locations, no warehouse stops - just field to table freshness.
