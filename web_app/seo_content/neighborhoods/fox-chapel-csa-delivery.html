Fox Chapel CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Fox Chapel?
Yes! We deliver throughout Fox Chapel Borough and surrounding areas including O'Hara Township. We can accommodate delivery to estate properties, gated communities, and all residential addresses. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
Is your produce truly organic?
We grow using sustainable, organic practices without synthetic pesticides or herbicides. While we focus on responsible growing practices rather than certification paperwork, our methods meet or exceed organic standards. Fox Chapel families can be confident in the purity and quality of our produce.
Can I add flowers to my CSA delivery?
Absolutely! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers weekly flower subscriptions that can be added to your vegetable CSA. Our seasonal arrangements are favorites among Fox Chapel members for both everyday elegance and entertaining.
Can deliveries be coordinated with household staff?
Yes! We're happy to coordinate delivery with your household staff, leave boxes in specific locations on your property, or follow any other instructions to ensure seamless service.
