Serving the Mt Washington Community

Mt Washington is Pittsburgh's crown jewel - a historic hilltop neighborhood with the most stunning views in the city. From the overlooks along Grandview Avenue to the charming streets that wind through this elevated community, Mt Washington residents appreciate the finer things in life.

That includes real, fresh food. The kind that actually tastes like something. The kind you can't get at a chain grocery store because it hasn't traveled 1,500 miles in a refrigerated truck.

Tiny Seed Farm brings that quality directly to your Mt Washington doorstep. We're not a tech platform or a middleman - we're the actual farmers who grew your vegetables, and we deliver them ourselves within 24 hours of harvest.

Mt Washington residents tell us they love the convenience of having truly local produce arrive at their homes. Whether you're near the top of the Duquesne Incline, along scenic Grandview Avenue, or tucked into the quieter streets of this historic neighborhood, we bring the farm to you every Tuesday.

Many of our Mt Washington members say cooking with our vegetables while looking out at the city skyline is one of life's simple pleasures. We're honored to be part of that experience.

Mt Washington CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Mt Washington?
Yes! We deliver throughout Mt Washington including Grandview Avenue, areas near both Inclines, Shiloh Street, Virginia Avenue, and all surrounding streets. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
What day do you deliver to Mt Washington?
Mt Washington deliveries happen every Tuesday between 10am and 6pm. You'll receive a notification when your box is delivered.
Do you use organic growing practices?
Yes! We use organic growing practices and never use synthetic pesticides or herbicides. Our vegetables are grown naturally with care for the land and your family's health.
Can you deliver flowers to Mt Washington?
Absolutely! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers fresh-cut flower delivery throughout Mt Washington. Add flowers to your CSA or order bouquets for special occasions - perfect for your home with those stunning views.
Can I skip weeks when traveling?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly - no charges for skipped weeks.
