East Liberty CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of East Liberty?
Yes! We deliver throughout East Liberty including areas near Bakery Square, Penn Avenue, and all residential streets. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm.
What day do you deliver to East Liberty?
East Liberty deliveries happen every Thursday between 10am and 6pm. You'll receive a notification when delivered.
Can you deliver to my apartment building?
Absolutely! We deliver to building entrances, lobbies (where permitted), or any location you specify. Many East Liberty members live in apartments and condos.
Can I skip weeks?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly.
