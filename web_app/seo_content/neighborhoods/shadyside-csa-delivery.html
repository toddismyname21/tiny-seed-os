Shadyside CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Shadyside?
Yes! We deliver throughout Shadyside including all residential areas. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
What day do you deliver to Shadyside?
Shadyside deliveries happen every Wednesday between 10am and 6pm. You'll receive a notification when delivered.
I live in an apartment. Can you still deliver?
Absolutely! We deliver to apartment building entrances and can follow specific instructions you provide. Many of our Shadyside members live in apartments.
Can I skip weeks when I'm traveling?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly.
