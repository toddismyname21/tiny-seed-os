Serving the Northside Community

Pittsburgh's Northside is a collection of vibrant neighborhoods rich with history, art, and community spirit. From the stunning Victorian architecture of the Mexican War Streets to the emerging food scene in Deutschtown, from the cultural attractions around Allegheny Commons to the energy of the North Shore near PNC Park and Acrisure Stadium - this is a community that values authenticity and local connections.

That's exactly what Tiny Seed Farm offers at Mayfly Market. We're not a tech platform or a grocery delivery app. We're a real family farm bringing vegetables we grew ourselves to a convenient Northside pickup location. When you walk through Allegheny Commons or catch a show at the Mattress Factory, you're supporting local Pittsburgh institutions. Our CSA extends that same commitment to your kitchen table.

Northside residents love the convenience of picking up truly local produce at Mayfly Market. Whether you're in a renovated Victorian in the Mexican War Streets, a walkup in Deutschtown, or a newer place near the North Shore, Mayfly Market is an easy stop on your way home.

Many of our Northside members work in the cultural institutions, stadiums, and businesses that make this area vibrant. They appreciate food that matches their values - local, sustainable, and connected to the community.

Northside CSA Pickup FAQ

Where do I pick up my CSA on the Northside?
CSA pickup for Pittsburgh's Northside is at Mayfly Market during regular market hours. It's centrally located and convenient for Mexican War Streets, Deutschtown, Allegheny West, Allegheny Commons, North Shore, Manchester, and surrounding areas.
What are the pickup hours at Mayfly Market?
Your CSA box will be available during Mayfly Market's regular operating hours. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 for specific times.
Can I add flowers to my CSA?
Yes! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers fresh-cut flower bouquets that can be added to your vegetable CSA. Pick up your flowers with your produce at Mayfly Market.
Can I skip weeks if I'm traveling?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly - no produce wasted, no charges for skipped weeks.
