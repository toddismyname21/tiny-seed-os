Cranberry Township CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of Cranberry Township?
Yes! We deliver throughout Cranberry Township including all neighborhoods and developments. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
How far is your farm from Cranberry Township?
Our farm is located in Rochester, PA - just a short distance from Cranberry Township. Your produce travels from field to your kitchen quickly, ensuring maximum freshness.
Can I add flowers to my CSA delivery?
Absolutely! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers weekly flower bouquets that can be added to your vegetable CSA. Many Cranberry Township members love having fresh, locally-grown flowers delivered with their produce.
Can I skip weeks when we're on vacation?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly - perfect for Cranberry Township families with busy summer schedules.
