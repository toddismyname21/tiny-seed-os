Serving North Hills Families

We're proud to deliver to families throughout the North Hills, one of Pittsburgh's most family-friendly regions. Our farm in Rochester is just north of the city, making us your closest connection to real local agriculture.

North Hills families tell us they love having truly local produce delivered right to their door - no need to drive to a farmers market or settle for grocery store vegetables that have traveled thousands of miles. Our vegetables are often in the field the morning before they arrive at your house.

From active families in Ross and McCandless to growing families in Pine and Wexford, North Hills neighbors are discovering the joy of farm-fresh vegetables delivered weekly. Your kids will actually want to eat these veggies!

North Hills CSA Delivery FAQ

Which North Hills communities do you deliver to?
We deliver throughout the North Hills including Ross Township, McCandless, Pine Township, Wexford, Cranberry, Hampton, Shaler, and surrounding areas. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your specific address.
What day do you deliver to the North Hills?
North Hills deliveries happen every Wednesday between 10am and 6pm. You'll receive a notification when your box is on its way and when it's delivered.
Is there an extra fee for delivery to the North Hills?
No! Delivery to all North Hills addresses is included in your membership price with no additional fees.
Can I skip weeks for family vacations?
Absolutely! Just let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Perfect for summer vacation schedules. Your membership extends accordingly.
