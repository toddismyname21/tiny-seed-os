Serving the South Side Community

Pittsburgh's South Side has always had its own vibe - independent, energetic, and unapologetically itself. From the legendary stretch of East Carson Street to the steep steps of the Slopes, this neighborhood attracts people who appreciate authenticity over pretense.

That's exactly what we deliver. Tiny Seed Farm isn't a tech company or a grocery delivery app. We're a real farm run by real people, bringing vegetables we actually grew to your South Side doorstep. No algorithms deciding what goes in your box - just a farmer who knows his fields and his customers.

Young professionals in the Flats tell us they love having fresh vegetables waiting when they get home from work. Families on the Slopes appreciate not having to carry grocery bags up those legendary steps. And everyone appreciates produce that actually tastes like something - not the flavorless stuff that's been trucked across the country.

Whether you're in a row house on Sarah Street, an apartment along East Carson, or a home tucked into the Slopes with views of the city, we bring the farm to you every Tuesday.

South Side CSA Delivery FAQ

Which South Side areas do you deliver to?
We deliver throughout Pittsburgh's South Side including South Side Flats (the East Carson Street area), South Side Slopes, and the South Side Works area. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your address.
What day do you deliver to the South Side?
South Side deliveries happen every Tuesday between 10am and 6pm. You'll receive a notification when your box is delivered.
Can you deliver to my apartment?
Absolutely! We deliver to apartments, row houses, and homes throughout the South Side. Just let us know your preferred drop spot - porch, building entrance, whatever works for you.
What about the Slopes? Those hills are intense.
We deliver to the Slopes! We know the steps, the hills, and the unique challenges. Just give us clear delivery instructions and we'll get your vegetables to your door.
Do you deliver flowers to the South Side?
Yes! Tiny Seed Fleurs offers fresh-cut flower delivery throughout the South Side. Brighten up your apartment or surprise someone special - locally grown flowers delivered with your vegetables or on their own.
