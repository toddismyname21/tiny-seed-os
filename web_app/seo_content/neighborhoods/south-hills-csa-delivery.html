Serving South Hills Families

We're proud to deliver to families throughout the South Hills, one of Pittsburgh's most established and family-oriented regions. From the tree-lined streets of Mt. Lebanon to the growing communities of Upper St. Clair and Bethel Park, we bring the farm to your doorstep.

South Hills families appreciate quality and value. Our CSA delivers both - vegetables so fresh they taste completely different from anything at the grocery store, at a price that makes sense for weekly meals. Friday delivery means you're set for weekend cooking and the week ahead.

Whether you're a longtime resident or recently moved to one of these wonderful communities, Tiny Seed Farm connects you directly to local agriculture without leaving your neighborhood.

South Hills CSA Delivery FAQ

Which South Hills communities do you deliver to?
We deliver throughout the South Hills including Mt. Lebanon, Bethel Park, Upper St. Clair, Dormont, Castle Shannon, Scott Township, Bridgeville, and surrounding areas. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to confirm your specific address.
What day do you deliver to the South Hills?
South Hills deliveries happen every Friday between 10am and 6pm. Perfect timing for weekend cooking!
Is there an extra fee for South Hills delivery?
No! Delivery to all South Hills addresses is included in your membership price with no additional fees.
Can I skip weeks?
Absolutely! Just let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly - you never lose value.
