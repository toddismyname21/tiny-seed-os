Serving the Lawrenceville Community

We're proud to offer CSA at the Tuesday Farmers Market in Lawrenceville, Pittsburgh's thriving neighborhood known for its independent spirit, creative energy, and appreciation for quality local goods. From the galleries of Butler Street to the row houses throughout Upper, Central, and Lower Lawrenceville, the Tuesday market is an easy stop.

Lawrenceville has always supported local makers and small businesses. Our CSA is a natural fit - direct from our family farm to your kitchen, with no middlemen, no warehouses, just fresh vegetables grown with care. Pick up your box while supporting other local vendors at the market.

Whether you're a chef looking for restaurant-quality ingredients for home cooking, a family wanting to eat healthier, or anyone who appreciates knowing exactly where their food comes from, Tiny Seed Farm has you covered at the Tuesday market.

Lawrenceville CSA Pickup FAQ

Where do I pick up my CSA in Lawrenceville?
CSA pickup for Lawrenceville is at the Tuesday Farmers Market during market hours. It's a convenient location for all of Upper, Central, and Lower Lawrenceville. Contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 for details.
What are the pickup hours?
Pickup is available during Tuesday Farmers Market hours. Your CSA box will be ready for you to grab while you browse other vendors.
Can I combine my CSA pickup with market shopping?
Absolutely! That's one of the best parts of market pickup. Grab your farm-fresh vegetables and explore other local vendors all in one trip.
Can I skip weeks?
Yes! Let us know by the weekend before and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends accordingly.
