{{NEIGHBORHOOD}} CSA Delivery FAQ

Does Tiny Seed Farm deliver to all of {{NEIGHBORHOOD}}?
Yes! We deliver throughout {{NEIGHBORHOOD}} including all residential areas. If you're unsure whether your specific address is covered, contact us at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 and we'll confirm.
What day do you deliver to {{NEIGHBORHOOD}}?
{{NEIGHBORHOOD}} deliveries happen every {{DELIVERY_DAY}}. Boxes typically arrive between 10am and 6pm. You'll receive a text notification when your delivery is on its way and when it's been dropped off.
Is there an extra delivery fee for {{NEIGHBORHOOD}}?
No! Delivery to {{NEIGHBORHOOD}} is included in your membership price. There are no hidden fees or delivery charges.
Where do you leave the CSA box?
We deliver to your doorstep, porch, or a location you specify. For apartments, we can deliver to building entrances or specific instructions you provide. All boxes are insulated to keep produce fresh.
Can I skip a week if I'm traveling?
Absolutely! Just let us know in advance (by the weekend before your delivery) and we'll skip that week. Your membership extends by that week, so you never lose value.
When does the CSA season run?
Our main season runs from late May through October (approximately 22 weeks). We also offer extended fall shares with storage crops. Sign-ups typically open in January and popular share sizes sell out by spring.

About Tiny Seed Farm

Tiny Seed Farm is a family-owned farm in Rochester, PA, just north of Pittsburgh. We've been growing vegetables for Pittsburgh families, building a community of members who value fresh, local food and genuine relationships with their farmer.

Unlike aggregator platforms that act as middlemen between you and anonymous farms, when you join Tiny Seed Farm's CSA, you're buying directly from us. You can visit our farm, call Todd with questions, and know exactly where every vegetable in your box was grown.

We deliver throughout the Pittsburgh metro area, including {{NEIGHBORHOOD}}, bringing farm-fresh produce directly to your door. No pickup locations, no warehouse stops - just field to table freshness.
