Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I pick up my CSA box in Pittsburgh?
Tiny Seed Farm offers CSA pickup at 15+ locations across Pittsburgh. Options include farm pickup at Kretschmann Family Organic Farm in Rochester, Allison Park pickups at St. Paul's and Simon's Produce Stand, farmers market pickups in Sewickley (Saturday), Lawrenceville (Tuesday), and Bloomfield (Saturday), local market pickups at Today's Organic Market (Oakmont), Mayfly Market (North Side), and Bryant St. Market (Highland Park), plus home delivery to neighborhoods including Mt. Lebanon, Squirrel Hill, Zelienople, North Park, Fox Chapel, and Cranberry Township.
What is the difference between CSA pickup and delivery?
CSA pickup locations allow you to collect your farm share at a convenient spot like a farmers market or local market during their operating hours. Home delivery brings your CSA box directly to your door on your scheduled delivery day. Both options get you the same fresh, farm-direct produce - choose based on what fits your schedule and location.
What days are CSA pickups available?
CSA pickup days vary by location. Farmers market pickups are available on Tuesdays (Lawrenceville) and Saturdays (Sewickley, Bloomfield). Local market pickups at Today's Organic Market, Mayfly Market, and Bryant St. Market are available during regular market hours. Home deliveries are scheduled on specific days by neighborhood.
Can I request a new CSA pickup location in my neighborhood?
Yes! Tiny Seed Farm adds new pickup and delivery locations when there are 15 or more interested members in an area. If you don't see your neighborhood listed, use the form above to submit your email and zip code - when we have enough interest, we'll add your area to our route.
Do all CSA locations have the same produce selection?
Yes! Every CSA pickup location and delivery receives the same high-quality, freshly-harvested produce. Your box contents are determined by your share size (Small, Standard, or Family) and what's in season, not by which location you choose. All produce is harvested within 24 hours of pickup or delivery.
How do I change my pickup location?
Just contact Todd at todd@tinyseedfarmpgh.com or call 717-725-5177 to change your pickup location. We're flexible - you can even switch between delivery and pickup options if your schedule changes.
