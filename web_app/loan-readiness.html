Tips for Effective Loan Documents

Business Plan Tips

  • Include specific revenue projections with data
  • Show your experience and qualifications
  • Detail your competitive advantages
  • Explain how you'll repay the loan
  • Include risk mitigation strategies

Marketing Plan Tips

  • Show diverse revenue streams
  • Include customer retention strategies
  • Detail your pricing strategy
  • Explain your unique selling proposition
  • Show growth trajectory with evidence

Lender Expectations

  • FSA wants to see beginning farmer focus
  • Horizon values production history
  • Banks want collateral details
  • All lenders want cash flow clarity
  • Include realistic assumptions
