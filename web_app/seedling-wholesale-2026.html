Frequently Asked Questions

Just 1 tray (18 plants). There is no minimum tray count to get started. Order 6+ trays to unlock Volume pricing, or 11+ for Bulk pricing.

Each tray is a standard nursery flat with 18 plants of a single variety. Seedlings are hardened off and ready to transplant on delivery day.

Delivery begins in April based on weather and variety readiness. We will contact you to schedule a specific delivery date that works for your operation. You can also pick up at the farm in Rochester, PA.
