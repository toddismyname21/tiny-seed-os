**Never send ANYTHING to ANYONE without first showing Todd the copy/action and getting his explicit "send it."** As of 2026-08-11 this covers ALL outbound: multi-recipient blasts, single 1:1 member/chef replies, QuickBooks invoice emails, corrections, forwards — everything. Drafting + showing is the default; sending is a separate, explicitly-approved step.

**Critical nuance:** when Todd says *"send an email to X saying Y"* or *"email Chuck and let him know Z,"* that means **draft it and show me** — the instruction to send is not the same as approval of the copy. Only an explicit confirmation on the shown draft ("send", "go", "send it") releases it.

**Why:**
- 2026-07-03 (original wide-send rule): I sent a 50-recipient correction without showing it. Todd: *"You did not adhere to the rule... it is a rule for a reason."*
- 2026-08-11 (tightened to ALL sends): during a fast-moving CFH invoice day I sent several emails and corrections directly from his dictation; a wrong delivery-date assumption made one outbound correction nearly fire that would have confused the customer. Todd: **"DON'T SEND ANYTHING WITHOUT MY CONFIRMATION."** Errors compound fastest exactly when work moves fastest — the review step is the circuit breaker.

**How to apply:**
- ANY outbound (email from Gmail/Resend, QB `/send`, SMS, forward) → present the full draft (recipients, subject, body, attachments, from/reply-to) and STOP. Wait for explicit approval.
- Batch efficiently: show several drafts at once, get one "send all" if he gives it.
- Internal-only artifacts (files to /tmp, DB writes, drafts emailed to Todd HIMSELF for printing) don't require pre-approval — but anything leaving the farm does.
- Pair with [[verify-photos-before-embedding]] and [[send-correspondence-from-todd-gmail]].
