**Before putting any photo into a customer-facing email as Tiny Seed's produce, confirm it's actually from the farm.** Look for tells that it's NOT ours: **price tags, store shelving/signage, packaging, barcodes**. When in doubt, ask Todd "is this your photo?" rather than assuming.

**Why:** 2026-07-03 — I embedded a photo into the wholesale availability blast (50 chefs) and called it "your broccoli looking great." It was actually a **grocery-store display Todd photographed to compare pricing for the farmers market** — it even had a "$5.99" price sign visible that I noticed and still didn't question. Required an immediate follow-up correction email to all 50.

**How to apply:**
- Photos going into a customer blast get a quick "is this the farm's?" gut-check first; flag anything with retail signage/price tags.
- This is part of the broader [[send-correspondence-from-todd-gmail]] / show-copy-before-blast discipline: for wholesale/CSA blasts, show the copy AND confirm any embedded images before sending.
- A little self-deprecating humor works well for Tiny Seed's audience when a correction IS needed — Todd's voice is warm and human.
