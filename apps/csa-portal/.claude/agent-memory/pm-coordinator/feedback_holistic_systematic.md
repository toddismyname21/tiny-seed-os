**Every change must be holistic and systematic — integrated end-to-end, never a tack-on.** Design the whole flow: creation → visibility at EVERY fulfillment point → resolution → verification. Reuse existing systems consistently across ALL relevant surfaces; do not add a feature to one screen and assume it works.

**Why:** Todd 2026-06-30, deeply frustrated. The makeup-promises system (`member_notices`) was wired into stop-manifest + pack-check but NOT the main **pack-sheet** the crew packs from — so owed add-ons (double mushroom/bread/cheese make-goods) got missed and were only discovered on/near pack day. His words: *"We can't keep thinking things are resolved and find out on pack day that they're not. These things need to be seen all the way through… When we make an update, it is holistic and systematic. We cannot tack things on and expect them to work. This is messing with my business and could cause me real problems."*

**How to apply:**
- Promises/makeups (`member_notices`) must **persist visibly until status=fulfilled** at every point they're acted on: pack-sheet, pick-pack, harvest/order totals, stop-manifest, pack-check. Same source of truth everywhere.
- When building anything, map the full lifecycle and wire it into ALL surfaces in one coherent pass — not one screen now and others "later."
- Factor open obligations into the upstream numbers too (e.g., add-on order counts + harvest must include owed makeups), not just the downstream display.
- **Verify end-to-end before calling it done.** Bias to "take your time, get it right" over fast partial delivery. Related: [[verify-before-done]] mindset.
