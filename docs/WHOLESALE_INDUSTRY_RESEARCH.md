# Wholesale Industry Research: Farm-Scale to Enterprise

**Research Date:** February 12, 2026
**Purpose:** Comprehensive analysis of wholesale management software from farm-scale operations to enterprise distributors (Sysco/US Foods level)

---

## Executive Summary

This research examines wholesale management solutions across the entire spectrum - from small farm operations to the world's largest food distributors. The philosophy driving this research: "We are not the big guys but we can glean useful info from the big guys. We have to assume they are doing the best because they have the most resources."

Key insight: Enterprise distributors like Sysco and US Foods have invested hundreds of millions in technology that smaller operations can learn from and adapt at scale.

## Part 5: Payment Terms & Invoicing

### Industry Standard: Net 30

**Definition:** Customer has 30 calendar days from invoice date to pay in full.

**Why It's Common in Wholesale:**
- Buyers can sell products before paying for them
- Balances seller cash flow with buyer flexibility
- Standard in manufacturing, wholesale, and B2B services

**Early Payment Discounts:**
- 2/10 Net 30 = 2% discount if paid within 10 days
- Incentivizes faster payment without forcing it

**Best Practices:**
1. Define terms clearly before work begins
2. Document in contracts/agreements
3. Specify due dates and late fees
4. List accepted payment methods
5. Consider late payment fees to discourage delays

**Cash Flow Reality:**
Sellers spend money for orders BEFORE receiving payment. Net 30 means effectively lending money to buyers for 30+ days.

## Conclusion

The wholesale software landscape offers robust solutions at every scale. The key lessons from enterprise distributors like Sysco and US Foods that apply to farm-scale operations:

1. **Mobile-first, offline-capable** - Orders should work anywhere
2. **Real-time visibility** - Inventory, delivery, order status
3. **Automation over manual** - Invoicing, reminders, order processing
4. **Data-driven insights** - Even simple analytics improve decisions
5. **Customer self-service** - 24/7 access reduces operational burden
6. **Integration ecosystem** - Connect accounting, routing, payments

The gap between farm-scale and enterprise is narrowing. Platforms like Local Line, Barn2Door, and Fresho are bringing enterprise-level features to operations of all sizes, making best-in-class wholesale management accessible to farms that prioritize the right technology investments.
