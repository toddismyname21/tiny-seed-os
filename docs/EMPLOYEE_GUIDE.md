# GETTING STARTED

## Your App

The **Employee App** is your main tool for daily work. Use it to:
- Clock in and out
- View your assigned tasks
- Mark tasks complete
- Log harvests
- Report problems

## First Login

1. Open `employee.html` in your browser
2. Enter your **4-digit PIN** (ask your supervisor if you don't have one)
3. Tap **Sign In**
4. You're in!

## Install on Your Phone

Adding the app to your home screen makes it faster to access.

### iPhone
1. Open the URL in **Safari** (important: use Safari, not Chrome)
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with up arrow)
3. Scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Name it (e.g., "Farm Tasks")
5. Tap **Add**

### Android
1. Open the URL in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **menu** (three dots, top right)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App**
4. Tap **Add**

Now you have an app icon on your home screen. Tap it to open the app instantly!

# TIME CLOCK

## Clocking In

### Requirements
- You must be **at the farm** (GPS verified)
- Your phone's location services must be **on**

### Steps
1. Open the app
2. On the Home tab, tap the big **CLOCK IN** button
3. Wait for GPS confirmation
4. You'll see "Clocked in at [time]"

### If Clock In Fails

**"Location required"**
- Make sure GPS/Location is on in your phone settings
- Make sure you're at the farm, not in the parking lot

**"Not in valid location"**
- Move closer to the main farm area
- If you're definitely at the farm, contact your supervisor

## Clocking Out

1. Open the app
2. Tap **CLOCK OUT** button
3. Confirm if prompted
4. You'll see your total hours for the shift

## Viewing Your Hours

- **Today's Hours**: Shows on the Home screen
- **This Week**: Shows below today's hours
- **Full History**: Go to More > Time History

## Important Time Clock Rules

- Clock in when you **start working**, not when you arrive
- Clock out when you **stop working**
- If you forget to clock in, tell your supervisor ASAP
- Do not clock in for breaks - just clock back in after

# VIEWING YOUR TASKS

## Task List

1. Tap the **Tasks** tab
2. See all tasks assigned to you
3. Tasks are sorted by priority (most urgent first)

## Task Card Information

Each task card shows:

```
+------------------------------------------+
| [85] Harvest Roma Tomatoes        [!TIME]|
|                                          |
| Location: Field 2, Beds 4-8              |
| Due: Today 2:00 PM                       |
| Est: 2 hours                             |
+------------------------------------------+
```

- **Priority badge** [85]: Urgency score (higher = do first)
- **Title**: What you need to do
- **Warning** [!TIME]: If there's a problem (see below)
- **Location**: Where to go
- **Due**: When it needs to be done
- **Est**: How long it should take

## Task Details

Tap any task card to see full details:
- Complete instructions
- Notes from supervisor
- Related crop information
- Start button to begin timing

# UNDERSTANDING PRIORITY BADGES

## What the Colors Mean

The colored badge shows how urgent the task is:

| Badge Color | Score | What It Means |
|-------------|-------|---------------|
| **Red** | 80-100 | Do this FIRST - very urgent |
| **Orange** | 50-79 | Should do today |
| **Yellow** | 30-49 | Important but flexible |
| **Green** | 0-29 | Do when you have time |

## At-Risk Warnings

If you see a yellow triangle warning, pay attention:

| Warning | What It Means | What to Do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| **[!TIME]** | Not enough time | Tell supervisor, may need help |
| **[!WEATHER]** | Weather will interfere | Do it now if possible |
| **[!OVERRIPE]** | Crop is ready now | Harvest immediately |
| **[!OVERDUE]** | Past due date | Do ASAP |
| **[!BLOCKED]** | Waiting on something | Check with supervisor |

## How to Prioritize Your Day

1. Look at your task list
2. Do **red** tasks first
3. Then **orange** tasks
4. Then **yellow** and **green**
5. Tasks with warnings need extra attention

If you're unsure what to do first, ask your Field Lead!

# COMPLETING TASKS

## Basic Completion

1. Tap the task you're working on
2. Do the work
3. Tap **Complete**
4. Done! Task moves to completed list

## With Time Tracking

For better tracking, use Start/Complete:

1. Tap the task
2. Tap **Start** when you begin
3. A timer starts automatically
4. Do the work
5. Tap **Complete** when finished
6. Your actual time is recorded

## Adding Notes

When completing, you can add notes:
1. Tap **Complete**
2. Type any notes (optional):
   - Issues you found
   - Quantity completed
   - Anything important
3. Tap **Submit**

## If You Can't Complete a Task

Sometimes you can't finish a task. Here's what to do:

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Need supplies | Tap task > Add note explaining > Tell Field Lead |
| Weather stopped you | Tap task > Add note > Task auto-adjusts |
| Not enough time | Tell Field Lead - may need reassignment |
| Don't know how | Ask Field Lead for instructions |
| Found a problem | Use Scout tab to report, then note on task |

# LOGGING HARVESTS

## When to Log

Log harvests **immediately after picking** - don't wait until end of day.

## How to Log

1. Go to the **Harvest** tab
2. Tap **Log Harvest**
3. Select the crop (scroll or search)
4. Enter quantity:
   - **Pounds** for most vegetables
   - **Count** for items like melons
   - **Bunches** for herbs, greens
5. Select quality (optional):
   - Grade A: Perfect
   - Grade B: Minor imperfections
   - Culls: Not sellable
6. Add notes if needed
7. Tap **Submit**

## Harvest Tips

- Weigh before washing if possible
- Use the closest scale
- If no scale, estimate and note it
- Log each picking session separately
- Be honest about quality grades

## Linking to Tasks

If your task was a harvest task:
1. Complete the harvest
2. Tap the task
3. Tap **Complete**
4. The harvest log and task link automatically

# FIELD SCOUTING

## What is Scouting?

Scouting means looking for problems in the field: pests, disease, weeds, irrigation issues, etc.

## Reporting Issues

### When You See a Problem

1. Go to **Scout** tab
2. Tap **Report Issue**
3. Select issue type:
   - Pest
   - Disease
   - Weed pressure
   - Irrigation problem
   - Equipment issue
   - Other
4. **Take a photo** (very helpful!)
5. Select location (field, bed)
6. Add description
7. Tap **Submit**

### Issue Types Guide

| Type | Examples | Look For |
|------|----------|----------|
| Pest | Aphids, beetles, worms | Holes in leaves, bugs on plants, eggs |
| Disease | Blight, mold, wilt | Spots, discoloration, wilting, fuzzy growth |
| Weed | Grass, bindweed | Weeds competing with crops |
| Irrigation | Dry spots, flooding | Wilting, puddles, broken lines |
| Equipment | Broken tools, tractor issues | Anything not working |

### Photo Tips

- Get close enough to see the problem
- Include context (whole plant + close-up)
- Make sure it's in focus
- Take multiple if needed

## Regular Scouting Tasks

If you're assigned scouting:
1. Walk assigned rows systematically
2. Check plants at multiple points
3. Report anything unusual
4. Complete the scouting task when done

# USING THE APP OFFLINE

## What Works Without Internet

If you lose cell signal in the field:

- View tasks you already loaded
- Mark tasks complete (syncs later)
- Start/stop time tracking
- Add notes to tasks

## What Needs Internet

- Clocking in/out (GPS verification)
- Submitting harvests
- Reporting scout issues
- Getting new tasks

## How Syncing Works

1. You complete tasks offline
2. App stores the data locally
3. When internet returns, data syncs automatically
4. You'll see a "Synced" message

## Preparing for Offline

Before heading to a dead zone:
1. Open the app with internet
2. Let all tasks load
3. Now you can work offline

## If Sync Fails

1. Return to an area with signal
2. Open the app
3. Wait for sync (may take a minute)
4. If problems persist, tell your supervisor

# TROUBLESHOOTING

## Common Problems

### "Cannot clock in - location required"

1. Open your phone's Settings
2. Find Location/GPS settings
3. Make sure Location is ON
4. Make sure the app/browser has location permission
5. Try again

### "Invalid PIN"

1. Make sure you're entering exactly 4 digits
2. Try again carefully
3. If still failing, your PIN may need reset - ask supervisor

### "Tasks not loading"

1. Check your internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh
3. Wait 30 seconds, try again
4. If still broken, close and reopen app

### "App is slow"

1. Make sure you have good cell signal
2. Close other apps on your phone
3. Refresh the page
4. If still slow, may be a system issue - report to supervisor

### "Can't submit harvest"

1. Check internet connection
2. Make sure all fields are filled
3. Try smaller numbers if entering large quantities
4. Refresh and try again

### "Task disappeared"

- It may have been reassigned
- Refresh your task list
- Check with your Field Lead

# FAQ

## Time & Clock

**Q: I forgot to clock in this morning. What do I do?**
A: Tell your supervisor immediately. They can adjust your time record.

**Q: Can I clock in from home?**
A: No, you must be at the farm. The app checks your location.

**Q: The app says I'm not at the farm but I am.**
A: Move to a more open area (GPS works better without buildings/trees). Try again in 30 seconds.

## Tasks

**Q: I finished my tasks early. What should I do?**
A: Check if there are more tasks assigned. If not, ask your Field Lead for more work.

**Q: A task isn't on my list but my supervisor told me to do it.**
A: Do what your supervisor says. They may add the task, or it may be informal work.

**Q: Why is a low-priority task at the top of my list?**
A: The AI calculates priority from multiple factors. A "low" manual priority might still be urgent due to weather or deadlines. Do tasks in the order shown.

## Harvests

**Q: I forgot to log a harvest from yesterday.**
A: Log it today but add a note that it was from yesterday. Tell your Field Lead.

**Q: I don't know the exact weight.**
A: Estimate and note that it's an estimate. Accuracy improves with practice.

## Problems

**Q: I found a pest I don't recognize.**
A: Take a good photo and submit via Scout. Someone will identify it.

**Q: My phone died in the field.**
A: When you get back, clock in manually (tell supervisor), then log your work.

**Q: The app crashed and I lost data.**
A: Data should sync automatically. Check your completed tasks. If something's missing, tell your supervisor.
