## 1. Per-Platform Posting Frequency Best Practices (2025-2026)

### Instagram

| Content Type | Recommended Frequency | Notes |
|--------------|----------------------|-------|
| Feed Posts (Photos/Carousels) | 3-5 per week | Quality over quantity; maintain 6-8 hour gaps between posts |
| Reels | 3-5 per week | Under 90 seconds performs best for engagement |
| Stories | Daily or almost daily | Keeps you top-of-mind; 2-3 hour spacing |
| Live | 1-2 times per month | Great for Q&A, virtual farm tours, harvest events |

**Key Insight:** Buffer's analysis of 2+ million posts found the more you post, the better content performs, but with diminishing returns. The algorithm favors regular activity and audience connection over volume.

**By Account Size:**
- Nano accounts (up to 10K followers): 2x per week
- Micro accounts (up to 100K): 3x per week
- Mid accounts (up to 500K): 5x per week

### TikTok

| Frequency Level | Posts/Week | Expected Impact |
|-----------------|------------|-----------------|
| Minimum | 2-3 | Baseline visibility |
| **Optimal (Recommended)** | **3-5** | 17% more views per post |
| Aggressive | 6-10 | 29% more views |
| Maximum | 11+ | 61% more views on best content |

**Key Insight:** Buffer's analysis of 11.4 million TikTok posts shows the most significant performance jump occurs between 1 post and 2-5 posts weekly. Daily posting shows diminishing returns beyond 5 posts/day.

**For Small Businesses:** 4-5 weekly posts delivers optimal results while allowing proper content planning.

### YouTube

| Content Type | Recommended Frequency | Notes |
|--------------|----------------------|-------|
| Long-form Videos | 1-2 per week | Focus on quality; consistency matters more than frequency |
| YouTube Shorts | 3-7 per week | Can scale to 1-3 per day if resources allow |
| Combined Strategy | 1 Long-form + 3-5 Shorts weekly | Channels using Shorts + long-form grow 41% faster |

**Key Insight:** Posting reliably once a week is far better than posting five Shorts one week and nothing for three weeks. A predictable schedule builds routine for both the algorithm and your audience.

### Pinterest

| Account Stage | Pins/Week | Pins/Day |
|---------------|-----------|----------|
| New accounts | 7-21 (1-3/day) | Focus on consistency |
| Growing accounts | 21-35 (3-5/day) | Build momentum |
| **Established (Recommended)** | **35-70 (5-10/day)** | Steady growth |
| Active/Large | 70-105 (10-15/day) | Maximum visibility |

**Key Insight:** Fresh content drives 90%+ of website traffic. The algorithm heavily prioritizes new pins (Creates) over saves. Do not pin more than 50 times a day.

**Seasonal Note:** Start posting 45-60 days before peak search times (e.g., Christmas content in October).

### Threads

| Account Stage | Recommended Frequency | Notes |
|---------------|----------------------|-------|
| New/Growing | 1-2 posts/week to 1-2 posts/day | Focus on quality |
| **Established (Recommended)** | **3-5 posts/week** | Sustainable, strong results |
| High Engagement | 1-3 posts/day | Maximum without audience fatigue |
| Aggressive Growth | Up to 10/day | Early stage traction building |

**Key Insight:** Posts have a shelf life of 60-90 minutes. The first 15-30 minutes determine everything - the algorithm tracks how quickly posts accumulate engagement.

### Facebook

| Strategy Level | Posts/Week | Posts/Day |
|----------------|------------|-----------|
| Minimum | 3-5 | - |
| **Optimal (Recommended)** | **7-14** | **1-2/day** |
| Aggressive | 14-28 | 2-4/day |

**Key Insight:** Brands that post daily often see a significant boost in interactions and reach. However, quality over quantity - ensure every post adds value.

## 2. Content Differentiation Strategy

### Platform-Specific Content Strengths

| Platform | Best Content Types | Audience Mindset | Content Tone |
|----------|-------------------|------------------|--------------|
| **Instagram** | Beautiful visuals, Reels, Stories, behind-the-scenes | Discovery, inspiration | Polished but authentic |
| **TikTok** | Raw/authentic video, trends, tutorials, farm life glimpses | Entertainment, quick learning | Casual, fun, trendy |
| **YouTube** | Educational long-form, tutorials, farm tours, how-tos | Deep learning, research | Informative, detailed |
| **Pinterest** | Seasonal inspiration, recipes, DIY, beautiful pins | Planning, future projects | Aspirational, helpful |
| **Threads** | Quick updates, thoughts, conversation starters | Real-time engagement | Conversational, reactive |
| **Facebook** | Community updates, events, longer posts, groups | Local community, older demo | Informative, community-focused |

### Cross-Posting vs. Unique Content Strategy

**Smart Cross-Posting Approach (Recommended):**

Instead of creating 100% unique content for each platform (unsustainable) or posting identical content everywhere (ineffective), use the **Hub-and-Spoke Model:**

1. **Create "Pillar" Content Monthly**
   - In-depth blog posts, detailed videos, comprehensive guides

2. **Break Into Platform-Specific "Spokes"**
   - Each pillar becomes 15-20 smaller pieces distributed across platforms

**Example for a "Dahlia Harvest" Pillar:**
| Platform | Content Adaptation |
|----------|-------------------|
| YouTube | 8-minute tutorial: "How We Harvest Dahlias for Maximum Vase Life" |
| TikTok | 30-second satisfying harvest clip with trending audio |
| Instagram Reel | 60-second harvest highlights with tips overlay |
| Instagram Feed | Carousel: "5 Signs Your Dahlias Are Ready to Cut" |
| Pinterest | Pin: "Dahlia Harvest Guide" infographic |
| Threads | Quick tip: "Pro tip: Harvest dahlias in the cool of morning..." |
| Facebook | Longer post with multiple photos and detailed explanation |

### Video Length Requirements

| Platform | Minimum | Optimal | Maximum |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|
| **TikTok** | 3 sec | 15-60 sec | 10 min (upload), 3 min (recording) |
| **Instagram Reels** | 3 sec | 15-90 sec | 20 min (but >3 min not shown to new audiences) |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 15 sec | 30-60 sec | 3 min (180 sec) |
| **Pinterest Video** | 4 sec | 15-30 sec | 5 min |
| **Stories (IG/FB)** | - | 15 sec segments | 60 sec |

**Best Practice:** Create videos at 30-60 seconds to work across all short-form platforms.

### Hashtag Strategies by Platform

| Platform | Optimal Count | Strategy |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| **Instagram** | 3-5 hashtags | Highly relevant, mix of niche + moderate reach; avoid 30-tag spray |
| **TikTok** | 3-5 hashtags | Mix broad trending (#FYP) with niche-specific (#FlowerFarm, #FungiFarming) |
| **YouTube** | 3-5 hashtags | Search-optimized; use in description |
| **Pinterest** | 2-5 keywords | Use in pin description naturally; keyword-focused |
| **Threads** | 0-3 hashtags | Minimal; focus on conversational content |
| **Facebook** | 1-3 hashtags | Optional; not algorithm-critical |

**Key 2026 Insight:** Instagram removed "follow hashtag" feature. Algorithms now prioritize SEO-friendly captions and AI-driven recommendations over traditional hashtag usage. Focus on keyword-rich captions.

## 3. Multi-Account Strategy for Instagram

### Managing @tinyseedfarm, @tinyseedfleurs, @tinyseedfungi

#### Account Purpose Differentiation

| Account | Focus | Target Audience | Content Pillars |
|---------|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **@tinyseedfarm** | Main hub, overall farm life, CSA, vegetables | Local customers, CSA members, general followers | Farm updates, CSA shares, seasonal produce, behind-the-scenes, family/team |
| **@tinyseedfleurs** | Cut flowers, arrangements, floral design | Brides, event planners, flower enthusiasts, DIY arrangers | Arrangements, growing tips, flower varieties, design tutorials, seasonal blooms |
| **@tinyseedfungi** | Mushroom cultivation, specialty fungi | Chefs, foodies, health-conscious consumers, mushroom enthusiasts | Growing process, varieties, recipes, health benefits, harvest shots |

#### Posting Frequency by Account

| Account | Feed Posts/Week | Reels/Week | Stories/Day |
|---------|-----------------|------------|-------------|
| @tinyseedfarm (Hub) | 4-5 | 3-4 | Daily |
| @tinyseedfleurs | 3-4 | 2-3 | 4-5x/week |
| @tinyseedfungi | 3-4 | 2-3 | 4-5x/week |

#### Cross-Promotion Best Practices

**Do:**
- Share/repost content from sub-accounts to main account Stories
- Tag sister accounts in relevant posts
- Create "From Our Sister Account" Story highlights
- Collaborate on Reels featuring multiple farm aspects
- Use main account to introduce/feature sub-accounts monthly

**Don't:**
- Post identical content across all three accounts
- Use the same hashtag sets for all accounts
- Cross-post every piece of content (audience fatigue)
- Neglect the distinct personality of each account

#### Content Ratio by Account (Modified 5-3-2)

**@tinyseedfarm (Main Hub) - Classic 5-3-2:**
| Type | Ratio | Content Examples |
|------|-------|------------------|

| Original/Brand | 30% | CSA updates, product features, farm news |
| Personal/Human | 20% | Team spotlights, family moments, farm dog content |

**@tinyseedfleurs (Product-Focused) - Modified 4-4-2:**
| Type | Ratio | Content Examples |
|------|-------|------------------|
| Curated/Educational | 40% | Flower care tips, design inspiration, seasonal guides |
| Original/Product | 40% | Arrangements, available blooms, wedding features |

**@tinyseedfungi (Niche/Educational) - Modified 5-3-2:**

## 6. Implementation Recommendations

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)
1. **Establish primary Instagram accounts** with consistent posting
2. **Set up Pinterest** - high-value for flower/food content, long content lifespan
3. **Create content batching system** - dedicate harvest days to content capture
4. **Implement scheduling tool** (Later, Planoly, or Social Champ)

### Phase 2: Expansion (Weeks 5-8)
1. **Launch TikTok** - repurpose best Reels, add trending content
2. **Start YouTube Shorts** - leverage existing video content
3. **Begin Facebook page** - focus on local community engagement
4. **Develop hashtag strategy** per platform

### Phase 3: Optimization (Weeks 9-12)
1. **Add Threads** - conversational, real-time content
2. **Analyze performance** - adjust frequencies based on data
3. **Begin YouTube long-form** - monthly educational videos
4. **Create cross-promotion system** between platforms

### Recommended Tools

| Purpose | Tool Options |
|---------|-------------|
| Scheduling | Later, Planoly, Social Champ, Hootsuite |
| Pinterest | Tailwind |
| Analytics | Native platform analytics + Sprout Social |
| Content Creation | Canva, CapCut, Adobe Express |
| Link Management | Linktree, Stan Store |

### Content Batching Strategy

**Weekly Content Capture Sessions:**
- Harvest days: Capture video clips, photos of produce/flowers
- Processing days: Behind-the-scenes, tutorials
- Delivery days: Customer reactions, CSA share features

**Monthly Content Planning:**
- Week 1: Plan content calendar for upcoming month
- Week 2-3: Execute planned content
- Week 4: Review analytics, adjust strategy
