## Key Insights for Your Presale

### Why This Research Matters

You're not competing with Big-Box Nurseries on photography. You're competing on:

1. **Authenticity** — USDA Organic seal, founder story, certified practices
2. **Selection** — "90+ heirloom varieties, all open-pollinated"
3. **Value** — "$6/seedling vs $12-15 big-box" + local pickup saves shipping
4. **Urgency** — Real deadline ("Order by April 2 for April 12-15 pickup"), not fake timers

### Design Stack (Zero Cost)

- **Typography:** Playfair Display (serif headings) + Inter (body) — free, Google Fonts
- **Colors:** Moss green + cream + terracotta — signals "organic, farm, artisanal"
- **Imagery:** CSS gradients + free SVG patterns from uuundulate/fffuel + Unsplash botanicals
- **Icons:** Emoji (🍅 🌿 🌶️) or free from FontAwesome
- **Animations:** Pure CSS entrance/hover effects, <200ms duration

### Conversion Optimization Priorities (in order)

1. **Above the fold (2 seconds):** Logo, "90+ varieties," "Order by April 2," CTA button, USDA seal
2. **Hero section:** Clear value prop, not beautiful imagery
3. **Product cards:** Icon + name + price + button, scannable in <5 seconds
4. **FAQ:** Answer "Why presale?" and "Why organic?" — this moves fence-sitters
5. **Trust signals:** Organic seal, certification name, years in business
6. **Mobile:** 44px touch targets, readable font size (16px min), <2s load time

### What NOT to Do

- ❌ Use fake countdown timers
- ❌ Show stock photos of farmers or nurseries
- ❌ Say "Limited supply" if you have 500 seedlings
- ❌ Use colors that feel corporate (bright blue, chrome gray)
- ❌ Load images larger than 50KB
- ❌ Skip the USDA seal—it's 70% recognized, converts automatically
