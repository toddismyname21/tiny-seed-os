# THE RECOMMENDATION: a written food safety plan and a validated wash-water sanitation program

**You are about to put every leaf you sell through a recirculating water system,
and you do not have a written sanitation program or a confirmed NOP-allowed
sanitiser.**

This is not a nice-to-have, and it is not me padding a budget. It is the one
thing in this analysis that could end the business rather than slow it down.

## Why this, and why now

**The investment itself creates the exposure.** Everything else in this budget
makes the product better. The chilled water loop makes it better *and* raises
risk, because water now touches every leaf and gets reused.

Penn State's own words, from the article this whole cooling plan is built on:

> *"Due to food safety concerns, hydrocooling is becoming **less widely used**
> because of the potential for water to **move pathogens among produce**."*

Their comparison table rates hydrocooling's **"Potential for Decay
Contamination" as HIGH** — the only cooling method so rated. And:

> *"If hydrocooling water is **recirculated**, it **should be chlorinated** (or
> other approved sanitizer) to minimize disease problems."*

**Recirculation is exactly what the loop does.** One contaminated batch reaches
the whole tank, and from the tank, every bag packed that day.

## The specific risk profile of this farm

| Factor | Why it compounds |
|---|---|
| **Ready-to-eat bagged leafy greens** | The **highest-risk produce category in the United States.** Bagged salad is why the FSMA Produce Safety Rule exists. Nobody cooks it |
| **Recirculated wash water** | Shared water is the classic vector — see Penn State above |
| **Food banks and institutions** | Center for Hope, Gleaner's. These buyers serve vulnerable people and are the **most likely to ask for documentation** |
| **Certified organic** | The sanitiser must be **NOP-allowed**. You cannot simply reach for what a conventional farm uses |
| **Seven SKUs, two markets, CSA, wholesale** | Wide distribution means a problem travels far before anyone notices |
| **Owner-operated** | No second person whose job is to catch it |

## 🔴 And this is already an open question I flagged twice and never closed
