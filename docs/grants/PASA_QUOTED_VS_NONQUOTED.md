# 3. ⚠️ 382 FENCE IS ON THE QUOTED LIST — so why did Luka say no?

Pasa's list shows **382 Fence, quoted**, defined as *"A constructed barrier to
animals or people."*

**But Todd was told fencing is not covered under 382, with leeway under 472
instead.**

🔴 **The second document explains it: PA lists 382 as "No Consult w/
Conditions."** There are **PA-specific conditions on Fence** that evidently
exclude our use — most likely because NRCS fence standards are written around
livestock management, and PA has attached conditions reflecting that.

✅ **472 Access Control is quoted AND "No Consult" with no conditions attached.**
It is the cleaner route for deer exclusion, and it is what Luka pointed at.

➡️ **Take 472. Do not argue for 382.**
