# ⚠️ Two notes on dropping the printer

**1. The labels line is the least certain number here.** $656.15 is 5,000 at
**$0.13/label — the verified UPrinting rate at 1,000 qty.** The true 5,000 rate
will be **lower**, so there is probably a little more room than shown. 🔴 **Still
needs a real 5,000-qty quote.**

**2. The risks the printer would have covered are now live:**

| Risk | Why it matters now |
|---|---|
| **Designs are not settled** | 5,000 pre-printed labels lock in a design |
| **OEFFA approval pending** | The USDA Organic seal cannot be altered and OEFFA may require approval before use. **Printing before sign-off risks the whole run** |
| **7 SKUs** | Cherry Tomato · Baby Arugula · Something Fresh Mix · Petite Kale Mix · Wild Arugula · Baby Spinach · **King Spring Mix**. 5,000 split seven ways is ~714 each, and **each design is its own print run with its own setup** — which is what kills the quantity break |
| **Moisture** | Refrigerated product still needs **poly/BOPP stock with a wet-strength, all-temperature adhesive.** Specify it on the order — paper labels will fail in the cooler |

➡️ **Mitigation that costs almost nothing:** print **King Spring Mix only first**
— it is the most popular — ~$131 for 1,000. It tests the design, the adhesive and
the cooler before the other six are committed.

🔴 **Ask Lauren Pope at OEFFA about label approval before any order is placed.**
