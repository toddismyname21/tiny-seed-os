✅ **Yes. A farm-written plan is the normal case, not a shortcut.** Auditors
expect the grower to write it — a plan written by someone who does not do the
work is worse, because nobody follows it.

# Start from a template, not a blank page

| Source | What it gives |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Produce Safety Alliance (Cornell)** | **free farm food safety plan templates and SOP forms.** The PSA is the FDA-recognised standard curriculum for the FSMA Produce Safety Rule. `producesafetyalliance.cornell.edu` |
| **PSA Grower Training** | one-day course, widely offered in PA. Often required or strongly expected. **Penn State Extension runs them** |
| **Penn State Extension** | PA-specific produce safety guidance and plan writing help |

➡️ **Ten hours gets a real first draft from a PSA template.** It will not produce
a polished GAP-audit manual — that is usually 20–40 hours and a mock audit. But a
first draft is what you need now, and the plan is the prerequisite to everything
else.

# What this plan must cover that a generic one will not

The farm is adding a **recirculating** chilled water loop. That is the section a
template will not write for you, and it is the whole reason this matters:

| Section | What has to be decided |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **Wash and hydrocooling water** | Which **NOP-allowed** sanitiser, at what concentration, how often tested, with what and by whom, and the log that proves it |
| **Water source testing** | baseline and in-season testing of the well, with records |
| **Water change frequency** | when the tank gets dumped and refilled |
| **Temperature monitoring** | the cold chain, which the Temp Stick loggers were specified for |
| **Lot coding** | the band sealer prints pack date and lot code — the plan says what the code means and how it traces back |
| **Worker health and hygiene** | standard, template covers it |
| **Equipment cleaning** | the tri-clamp manifold was chosen *because* it comes apart. Say so — it is a strength |

## 🔴 The one question to get answered before writing that section

**Which sanitisers are allowed under the National Organic Program for produce
wash water, and at what concentration?**

I have flagged this three times now and it is still unverified. **Do not write a
number into the plan from memory or from a conventional source.**

➡️ **Ask Lauren Pope at OEFFA.** It is one email, the answer is specific, and it
has to be in the Organic System Plan anyway.
