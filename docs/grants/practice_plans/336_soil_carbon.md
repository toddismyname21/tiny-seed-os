## 1. What it is

Adding a stable carbon material to soil — compost, biochar, or similar — to
build organic matter and hold carbon in the ground.

## 2. What we physically do

### The easy version — compost, onsite ($320.99/ac)

**Already happening.** You make compost and spread it. 5 acres = **$1,605.**

⭐ **Stacks with 317 Composting Facility** (the pad) and **590 Nutrient
Management** (the plan that sets the rate). Three practices, one system.

### ⭐ The interesting version — biochar ($1,712.13/ac)

**Biochar is charcoal made for soil** — biomass pyrolysed at low oxygen. It is
extremely stable, holds water and nutrients, and hosts microbial life. **It is
also expensive, which is exactly why a farm never buys it.**

5 acres at $1,712.13 = **$8,561.** That is real money for a genuinely good
long-term soil input.
