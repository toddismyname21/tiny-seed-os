## 1. What it is

No-till is stricter than 345 Reduced Till: **the soil is not inverted or stirred
at all** except in the narrow slot where seed goes.

## 2. What we physically do

| | |
|---|---|
| Terminate the previous crop | **flail mow + tarp**, never tillage |
| Plant | direct-seed into the undisturbed surface, or transplant into a dibbled hole |
| Between crops | the soil surface stays covered — residue, cover crop, or tarp |

**Where:** the blocks with the cleanest termination. **Start smaller than you
think.** No-till organic is demanding; prove it on two or three blocks before
committing acres.

## 3. ⚠️ The honest difficulty

**This is the hardest practice on the list to do well organically.** Without
tillage you lose your weed reset, and you are relying entirely on occultation and
timing. Farms that go no-till organic without a plan get buried in weeds in year
two.

**That is precisely why it pays $3,631 flat and why "Adaptive Management" is the
variant.** The money is for the risk and the thinking, not the equipment.
