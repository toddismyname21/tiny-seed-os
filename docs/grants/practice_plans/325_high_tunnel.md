## 1. What it is

An unheated, covered structure growing crops **in the ground** — not a
greenhouse with benches. Extends the season at both ends, keeps rain off the
foliage, and lets you grow through months when the field is dead.

## 2. What we physically do

You already run tunnels, so this funds **a new one, or a replacement**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Site it | level-ish, drains, in **FSA Field 1 or 2**, oriented for winter light |
| Build | ground posts, bows, purlins, poly, endwalls, roll-up sides |
| Grow | ⭐ **winter salad greens** is the obvious use — your flagship crop, in the months you cannot field-grow it |

⭐ **The AIG connection:** Round 2 requested **$12,668 of greenhouse and
high-tunnel automation from Nolt's** and appears to have been cut. **325 is on
Pasa's list.** The conservation side may fund what the state program declined.

## 5. Practical notes

⚠️ **Tunnel soil needs managing.** No rain means salts accumulate. Plan for
periodic leaching or open-sided flushing — and record it, because it connects to
**590 Nutrient Management**.

⭐ **Pairs with 374 Energy** — a high-efficiency exhaust fan or an automatic
controller in the tunnel is a **separate** payment under 374.
