🔴 **This is the single most financially important unanswered question in the
plan — $4,458 per acre.**

## 1. The cost is verified, not estimated

Silage tarp runs **$0.08–$0.10 per square foot** (Carolina Tarps 32'×150' at
$385 = $0.080; Farm Plastic Supply 30'×100' at $289.99 = $0.097). An acre is
43,560 sq ft. **At $0.09 that is $3,920 per acre.**

## 2. What it is

Covering the soil to suppress weeds, hold moisture and moderate temperature.
NRCS counts **synthetic** material, not only straw and woodchips — which is the
finding that put tarps in Conservation rather than Business Development.

## 3. What we physically do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Occultation** — black silage tarp | laid after flail mowing. Weeds germinate in the dark and die. 2–4 weeks |
| **Solarisation** — clear tarp | mid-summer only. Traps heat, cooks weed seed and some pathogens |
| **Cycle** | tarp on → tarp off → plant into a clean, undisturbed bed |

**Where:** salad blocks in **FSA Field 1**, tomato ground in **Field 2**.

**Scale:** 3 acres of beds is a realistic first commitment.

## 4. What we buy

| | |
|---|---|
| Black silage tarp, 5 mil | $0.08–$0.10/sq ft ✅ |
| Clear solarisation film | ⚠️ **not yet priced** — different product |
| Sandbags or equivalent | edges are where tarps fail in wind |
| A reel or winder | ⚠️ not sourced. A wet tarp rolled badly fails early |

⚠️ **Silage tarp is not sold in 6-ft widths** — it comes 30', 32', 50'. Bed-width
strips mean seaming or custom order. **Block coverage over several beds has 8
edges instead of 24, and edges are the failure point.**

## 5. 🔴 What we record

| Record | When |
|---|---|

| Tarp log — **on date, off date, bed, material** | every cycle |
| Weed assessment before and after | every cycle |
| Soil temperature under clear film, if solarising | during |
| ⭐ **ADJUSTMENT** — *"3 weeks was not enough in April, so spring occultation moved to 5 weeks"* | whenever |

## 6. 🔴 THE TWO QUESTIONS

1. **"Does our bed-scale tarping fall under 'Synthetic Material' or 'Synthetic
   Material, Row'?"** — worth **$4,458 per acre**
2. **"Can 484 be claimed in more than one year?"** — tarps last 3–5 seasons. If
   the payment recurs while the tarp does not need replacing, the economics
   change completely

## 7. Practical note

🔴 **Do not buy tarps until the CPA-52 is approved.** USDA: *"Producers cannot
implement practice before an Environmental Evaluation is performed."*
