## 1. What it is

Reducing how much you disturb the soil, and managing the residue left behind —
under a **written plan**, with **monitoring**, and with **documented adjustment**
when things do not go as expected.

The "Adaptive Management" part is not decoration. It is the difference between
$6,075 and $0.

## 2. What we physically do

**This is the tarping plan, described in NRCS language.** We already intend it.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Crop finishes | instead of tilling it in, **mow with the flail** and leave residue on the surface |
| Terminate | **lay silage tarp** over the bed — occultation. 2–4 weeks depending on season |
| Pull tarp | residue has broken down, weeds have germinated and died, bed is plantable |
| Replant | direct-seed or transplant into the bed with minimal disturbance |

**Where:** start on the salad blocks inside **FSA Field 1 (1068/446/1)**. Those
are the fastest-turning beds, so they generate the most data fastest.

**Scale:** 3 acres of production beds is a credible first-year commitment.

## 3. What we buy

**Nothing for this practice.** The flail mower is in the Business Development
budget; the tarps go under practice 484. This practice pays for the *management
decision*, not the hardware.

## 4. 🔴 What we record — THIS IS THE PRODUCT

| Record | When | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Written tillage plan** — which beds, what reduction, why | **before the season** | farm OS |
| Baseline — how the ground was worked previously | once, at start | farm OS |
| **Date-stamped operation log** — every pass, by bed: flail, tarp on, tarp off, seed | each event | farm OS |
| **Residue/soil observations** — cover remaining, soil condition, moisture | monthly in season | photos + notes |
| **Weed pressure** — rated the same way each time | each turnover | simple 1–5 scale is fine |
| ⭐ **ADJUSTMENT record** — *"bed F3L had poor termination at 3 weeks in cool weather, so we extended to 5 weeks on later plantings"* | whenever it happens | **the part most farms miss** |

**The adjustment record is literally what "adaptive" means.** Without it this is
just reduced tillage.

weed reset and take on real risk. **That is what the payment is for** — it
compensates management burden and risk, not materials.
