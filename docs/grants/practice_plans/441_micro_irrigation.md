## 1. What it is

Delivering water directly to the root zone at low pressure and low volume — drip
tape, drip line with emitters, or microjets — instead of overhead.

## 2. What we physically do

Drip is already in use. This would fund **expansion, replacement, or an upgrade
to a better-designed system.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Surface PE with emitters** | heavier-wall polyethylene line with built-in emitters. Lasts years, unlike seasonal tape |
| **Microjet** | small spray emitters. Better suited to tomatoes and tunnels than to dense salad beds |
| Supporting | filtration, pressure regulation, zone valves, header layout |

**Where:** **FSA Field 1** salad blocks, **Field 2** tomatoes, and the tunnels.

## 3. ⭐ The AIG connection

AIG Round 2 requested a **$10,195 Toro Tempus Ag smart irrigation system** and
appears to have been cut — the award came in $15,323 under the ask. **Micro
Irrigation 441 is on Pasa's conservation list.** The conservation side may fund
what the state program declined.

⚠️ **Do not fund the same item twice.** If AIG did approve Toro, this must be a
different system on different ground.

## 4. 🔴 What we record

| Record | When |
