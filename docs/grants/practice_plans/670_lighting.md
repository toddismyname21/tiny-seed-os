# 670 — Lighting System Improvement

| Scenario (HU) | Pays |
|---|---|
| **LED Dimmer or Controller** | **$505.28 / Ea** |
| *(other LED scenarios exist — ask for the full list)* | |

⭐ **Flat, per item.** Low effort, stacks cleanly with **374 Energy Efficiency**.

## 1. What it is

Replacing inefficient lighting with LED, and adding controls — dimmers, timers,
occupancy sensors, photoperiod controllers.

## 2. What we physically do

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Greenhouse / propagation** | ⭐ photoperiod or supplemental lighting with a controller. You start everything from seed — light management is real here |
| **Pack house / wash station** | LED fixtures over the bagging table and wash line. ⭐ **Good light is a food-safety issue** — you cannot inspect what you cannot see |
| **Walk-in coolers** | LED with door switches |
| **Barn / storage** | fixtures and occupancy sensors |

## 3. 🔴 The connection worth making

**The food safety plan will require adequate lighting over food-contact
surfaces.** A GAP auditor checks it. **670 pays to fix it.**

➡️ **Propose 670 alongside 575 Trails and the food safety plan.** All three are
the same argument: a clean, well-lit, hard-surfaced pack area.

## 4. What we record

| Record | When |
|---|---|
| **Lighting inventory** — ⭐ *do this on the same walk as the 374 energy inventory* | before site visit |
| Baseline — existing fixture type, wattage, count | before |
| Install log — make, model, wattage, date, location | at install |
| Energy use after | following season |

## 5. Practical note

⭐ **Count the controllers, not just the fixtures.** The published scenario is
**"LED Dimmer or Controller" at $505.28 each** — so a greenhouse photoperiod
controller, a pack-house occupancy sensor and a cooler door switch are
potentially **three separate payments.**

**Add lighting to the pre-site-visit walk** described in `374_energy.md`.
