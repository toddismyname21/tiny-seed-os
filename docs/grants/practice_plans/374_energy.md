## 🔴 THE ACTION — do this before the site visit

**Walk the farm with a notebook and list every one of these:**

| Walk | Looking for |
|---|---|
| **Greenhouse** | exhaust fans (measure the diameter), any controller/thermostat, heater, bench length for root-zone heat |
| **Walk-in coolers** | compressor type and age, controller, fans |
| **Pack house** | motors on the spinners, any pump, lighting |
| **Irrigation** | pump motor horsepower, is there a VSD |
| **New build** | the chilled water loop's circulating pump — **a VSD on it is $1,458.80** |

**For each item write: what it is, horsepower or diameter, age, and whether it is
being replaced.** Each line on that list is potentially its own payment.

## What it is

Replacing energy-consuming farm equipment with higher-efficiency versions.
Payments are per item, flat, and **stack**.

## 🔴 What we record

| Record | When |
|---|---|
| **Energy inventory** — the walk above | before the site visit |
| Baseline — nameplate data on what is being replaced, utility bills if available | before |
| Install records — make, model, efficiency rating, date | at install |
| ⭐ **Energy use after** — the proof the practice worked | following season |

## Practical notes

⭐ **Seedling bench root-zone heat at $3.22/linear foot.** A propagation house
with 300 linear feet of bench is ~$966, and bottom heat materially improves
germination on a farm that starts everything.

⚠️ **Leased land again** — anything bolted to a building needs Don's agreement.

⚠️ *"Alley Scraper System"* tops the list at $32,339 but is a **dairy manure**
system. Not applicable. Do not let it distract.
