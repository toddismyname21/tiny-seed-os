## ⭐ Why this is on the list at all

**96-inch woven wire is deer fencing.** Nothing else needs to be eight feet tall.

**Deer are a real and expensive threat to salad greens** — a herd can take a
block of young greens in a night, and there is no organic rescue for it. Fencing
is also exactly the sort of capital cost that **farm cash never covers**: it is
too expensive to find in an operating budget and too unglamorous to prioritise.

**This is the clearest example of Todd's own framing — "cost prohibitive
practices that are better for the farm."**

## What we physically do

| | |

**Where here:** the salad blocks in **FSA Field 1** are the ones worth protecting
— highest value per square foot and the most attractive to deer.

## What it is worth doing the arithmetic on

A rectangle enclosing roughly 5 acres has a perimeter of about 1,900 feet →
**roughly $15,500 at the published rate.**

⚠️ **I do not know the real installed cost of 96" woven wire in PA.** It must be
priced before this is proposed. The rate may cover it comfortably or may not.
