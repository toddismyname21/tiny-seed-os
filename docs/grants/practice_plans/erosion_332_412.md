## ⭐ Why these need no argument

**100% of your FSA cropland is classified Highly Erodible Land.** All 44.05
acres. The cropland indicator is set on exactly the four HEL fields and nowhere
else.

**That is USDA's own classification, not a claim you have to make.** Erosion is
the documented resource concern on every acre you crop, and these two practices
are the direct response to it.

## 332 — Contour Buffer Strips

**What it is:** strips of permanent vegetation running **across the slope, on the
contour**, between cropped strips. They break the slope length so water never
builds enough speed to carry soil.

⭐ **The "Specialty Crops" scenario pays $2,203.40/ac** — the highest per-acre
rate relevant to this farm, because the foregone income from vegetable ground is
valued far above commodity ground.

**Where here:** **FSA Field 1 (1068/446/1)** — 33.55 acres of HEL, the largest
piece of cropland on the operation.

**What we do:** a conservation planner lays out the contour lines; strips are
seeded to permanent grass; cropping continues between them. ⚠️ **This must be
laid out properly — on the contour, not by eye. That is what the TAP is for.**

## 412 — Grassed Waterway

**What it is:** a shaped, grassed channel following the natural drainage line, so
concentrated water flows over grass instead of cutting a gully through the field.

**Where here:** wherever water concentrates after a heavy rain. ⚠️ **I do not
know where that is.** Ask on the site visit — *"where does the water run after a
big storm?"* Every farmer knows; it rarely gets written down.

**What we do:** shape the channel, seed to sod-forming grass, maintain it, and
never crop through it.

🔴 **"With checks" means structures** — rock or similar across the channel to slow
flow. **That is soil disturbance**, so the CPA-52 will scrutinise it.

## 🔴 What we record

| Record | When |
|---|---|
| **Layout map** — the TAP produces it; contour lines or channel alignment | before |
| Seeding log — species, rate, date | at establishment |
| **Establishment photos**, same spot each year | annually |
| **Erosion observations** — rills, gullies, sediment at the bottom. ⭐ before and after is the whole point | after heavy rain |
| Maintenance log — mowing, repair | as done |

## Practical notes

⚠️ **These take ground out of production permanently.** The foregone-income rate
is the compensation. On 7.6 production acres, think carefully about how much.

⚠️ **Leased land** — permanent strips change how the field is farmed. Don needs
to agree.

⭐ **Pair with 386 Field Border.** Borders at the edges, buffer strips through the
middle, both paying foregone income, both catching sediment on HEL ground.
