# 1. ⭐ THE CORE INSIGHT — your farm is small, so chase FLAT payments, not acres

Tiny Seed has **7.6 acres of production beds.** Per-acre conservation payments
are therefore worth very little here. **But many NRCS scenarios pay a flat amount
per instance, and those do not care how big the farm is.**

The same practice, on the same ground, pays wildly differently depending on which
scenario you enrol under:

| Practice | Per-acre route | Value | **Flat route** | **Value** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **340 Cover Crop** | Single Species, Organic × 6 ac | $741 | **Adaptive Management** | **$6,075** |
| **345 Reduced Till** | Non-Mechanical × 3 ac | $5,068 | **Adaptive Management** | **$6,075** |
| **329 No-Till** | No-Till & Strip-Till × 3 ac | $96 | **Adaptive Management** | **$3,631** |
| **590 Nutrient Mgmt** | basic | — | **Adaptive Nutrient Management** | **$2,999** |

**Cover Crop pays 8× more under Adaptive Management than per-acre — for the same
cover crop on the same beds.**

➡️ **Strategic rule #1: for every practice, ask the TAP which scenario pays flat,
and whether we qualify for it.**

> ⚠️ **I have NOT verified what NRCS requires for "Adaptive Management."** It
> typically means a written plan, monitoring, record-keeping and documented
> adjustment based on results. **That is a real obligation, not a loophole.**
> ⭐ **But it is one this farm is unusually well placed for** — Tiny Seed already
> runs a farm management system with field records, soil tests and harvest data.
> Most farms applying for this have a notebook. **Ask Luka what Adaptive
> Management requires, and whether existing records satisfy it.**

## ⚠️ THE QUESTION THAT DECIDES EVERYTHING

**Is there a per-farm cap on AMP conservation?**

I searched all three program documents — Pasa's deck, USDA's AMP FAQ, and the BD
overview. **No per-farm conservation cap is stated anywhere.** And USDA's FAQ says
outright:

> *"producers are **not bound by payment limitations or the adjusted gross income
> (AGI) limitations**."*

Compare that to the BD side, which documents its limits plainly — $15,000 soft
ceiling, $10,000 per item. **The absence of a stated conservation cap is
conspicuous.**

🔴 **But absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.** Pasa may hold an
internal per-farm budget, and the case study's $20,000 may be typical or may be
the practical limit.

➡️ **This is the first question for Luka, and the whole strategy depends on the
answer:** *"Is there a per-farm cap on conservation practice payments, and what
is a realistic range for a farm our size?"*
