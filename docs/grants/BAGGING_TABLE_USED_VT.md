## 🚨 THE THING TO CHECK FIRST — does it need an air compressor?

Those pneumatic fittings and the two-button control box strongly suggest the bag
holders are **air-actuated**. If so:

- **The table needs compressed air at the pack station to work as designed.**
- Do we have a compressor there? What CFM and pressure does it want?
- If we do not, that is an added cost — and an added line we would need to
  justify under the grant.
- **Or** it may work fine as a plain gravity table with the pneumatics unused.

**Ask the seller directly: "What do the buttons do, and does it need shop air?"**
That single answer decides whether this is a $1,500 table or a $1,500 table plus
a compressor.

## 🚨 Three things to settle BEFORE buying

### 1. Is used equipment eligible?
The Business Development Overview does not say either way. Many programs require
new, or allow used with documentation. **Ask Chris Esposito before paying.** If
used is not eligible, this is a farm purchase, not a grant line — still a good
buy, just not with this money.

### 2. Will a Marketplace listing work as an "estimate"?
Chris's email accepts *"a screenshot of an online shopping cart, or even a simple
price quote."* **A Marketplace screenshot probably qualifies** — but the listing
will disappear once sold, so **screenshot it now**, with the price, description
and date visible.

### 3. Ask the seller the questions the photos will not answer
- [ ] ⭐ **What do the red and green buttons do, and does it need shop air?**
      The photo shows pneumatic fittings at the funnel.
- [ ] **Are there scales?** The photo shows none.
- [ ] **What else are you selling?** There is a conveyor and a hopper behind it
      in the photo — this may be a whole pack line coming apart.
- [ ] **How long has it been outside?** Stainless is fine; the controls may not be.
- [ ] **What are the bag opening / funnel diameters?** Ours must suit ¼ lb, 1 lb
      and 3 lb bags.
- [ ] Is it **food-grade 304 stainless**, and what condition are the welds and
      surfaces in? Pitting matters for a food-contact surface and for GAP.
- [ ] **Why are they selling** — farm closing, upgrade, or does something not work?
- [ ] Will they **hold it** while we confirm grant eligibility?
- [ ] Exact weight and whether it **breaks down** for transport. A 6×4 table may
      or may not go in the ProMaster assembled.
