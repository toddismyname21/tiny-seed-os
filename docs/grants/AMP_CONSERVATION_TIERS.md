## 🔴 THE RECORDS TO START KEEPING NOW

**"Adaptive Management" is where the money is, and it is the variant that demands
documentation.** I have not verified NRCS's exact requirement — ask Luka — but
every Adaptive Management scenario across every program rests on the same four
things:

| What to record | For which practice |
|---|---|
| **A written plan, before the season** — what you intend, where, why | 340, 345, 329, 590 |
| **Field-by-field, date-stamped activity log** — what was planted/tarped/applied, when, on which FSA field | all |
| **Monitoring data** — soil tests, observations, weed pressure, cover establishment | 340, 345, 590 |
| **Documented ADJUSTMENT** — "we saw X, so we changed to Y." ⭐ *This is the part most farms miss, and it is the literal meaning of "adaptive"* | all |

⭐ **This farm has an unfair advantage here.** Tiny Seed already runs a farm
management system with field records, soil tests and harvest data. **Most
applicants bring a notebook.** Ask Luka whether existing records satisfy the
requirement — if they do, Tier 1 is close to free money.

**Tier 1 flat payments alone: ~$25,355.** That exceeds Pasa's entire case-study
award, for practices already in the ground.
