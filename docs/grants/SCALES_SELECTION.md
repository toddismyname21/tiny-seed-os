## The tradeoff, which is physics not shopping

| SellEton SL-915-Series | $352.00 | 12×12 | 100 lb | 0.02 | **8.0%** ❌ |
| VisionTechShop TBWS-100 | $675.99 | **15.7×19.6** | 100 lb | 0.02 | **8.0%** ❌ |
| 24×24 Weigh Master | $845.00 | 24×24 | 1000 lb | 0.005 | 2.0% |

**Resolution is a function of capacity.** A 60 lb scale reading 0.002 lb is
30,000 divisions — high precision. A 100 lb scale reading 0.02 lb is 5,000. The
big cheap platforms are not bad scales; they are coarse because they are rated
for far more weight than we will ever put on them.

**Your heaviest bag is 3 lb — a 60 lb scale is already 20× that.** Your lightest
is **¼ lb, and that is what needs the resolution.** At 0.02 lb the scale sees
your quarter-pound bag in 8% steps, which means giving product away on every one.

So: **take the resolution, accept the 10×10 platform.** A 3 lb bag of greens sits
on 10×10 fine.
