# 2. THE PROCESS — Pasa's own "Farmer Journey"

1. **Apply** at `pasafarming.org/programs`
2. **Centralized intake and FSA eligibility check** ← *you have done this*
3. **Project planning with a Technical Assistance Provider** ← *Luka*
4. **Site visit** — site evaluated, practices mapped
5. 🔴 **CPA-52 Environmental and Cultural Resource Review (NRCS)** — the TAP writes it and submits to state NRCS
6. **Project implementation + participation stipend + 80% practice payment**
7. **Practice verification**
8. **Final 20% payment**

## 🔴 THIS SETTLES THE TARP QUESTION, DEFINITIVELY

USDA's FAQ, verbatim:

> *"**Producers cannot implement practice before an Environmental Evaluation is
> performed.**"*

**Do not buy or lay tarps until the CPA-52 is approved.** I guessed at this
earlier as "probably." It is not probably — it is the rule, in writing, from
USDA.

➡️ **The CPA-52 is now the critical path for the entire conservation side.**
Luka writes it. Everything else waits on it.

# 3. ⭐⭐ THE CASE STUDY — a farm almost exactly like yours

Pasa's deck includes a worked example. Their description of the farm:

> *"Between **2–7 acres** · **Organic vegetables** · Incubator farm · **Low
> budget, big goals**"*

**That is Tiny Seed's shape** — 7.6 acres of production beds, certified organic
vegetables.

## What they got approved — SEVEN practices

| Practice | What it did |
|---|---|
| **484 Mulching** | **reducing weed pressure** |
| **340 Cover Crop** | increasing organic matter |
| **595 Pest Management Conservation System** | **beneficial insects and insect netting** |
| 612 Tree/Shrub Establishment | planting fruit trees |
| **327 Conservation Cover** | pollinator habitat |
| **636 Water Harvesting Catchment** | water storage for irrigation |
| **374 Energy Efficient Agriculture Operation** | greenhouse insulation |

## What they were paid

| | |
|---|---|
| Up front | **~$16,000** |
| On verification | **$4,000** |
| **Total practice payments** | **$20,000** |
| Plus | **$1,500 stipend, yearly** |

⭐ **Note the first two lines.** Mulching for weed pressure and Pest Management
for insect netting are **exactly what Todd has been planning for months**, and
they were approved for a farm of this size.

## ⚠️ One rate I deliberately did NOT use

484 has two synthetic scenarios that disagree sharply:

| Scenario | Rate | Per acre |
|---|---|---|
| **Synthetic Material** | $3,003.55 / **Ac** | $3,003.55 |
| Synthetic Material, **Row** | $171.29 / **kSqFt** | **$7,461.39** — 2.5× |

**"Row" is plainly a different, more intensive application** — laying and
removing row plastic. Using it farm-wide would have shown $22,387 for the tarps
instead of $9,010. **I used the lower one.** Ask Luka which applies; if it is the
Row scenario, the number goes up substantially.
