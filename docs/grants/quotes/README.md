### Why — and the mistake I made

**CMI builds for commercial food processors, not farms.** Their catalogue is
onion peelers at 120 onions/minute, retorts, blanchers, metal detectors. The
Model 20 "small" spinner is a 4 cu ft machine with variable-speed drive,
electric braking and an RPM counter. That is a factory machine.

**My estimates were badly wrong — 5× to 9× low.** I priced them as farm
equipment because the farm needed farm equipment, rather than checking what tier
of supplier CMI actually is. The tell was in their own catalogue and I read past
it.

**What should have warned me:** Charlie's Triple Wash System doing **2,000 lb of
loose-leaf lettuce per hour** against a farm washing a few hundred pounds a day.
That scale mismatch was visible on day one.
