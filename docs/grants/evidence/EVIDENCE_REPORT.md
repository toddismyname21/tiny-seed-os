# 1. 🔴 START HERE — PASA'S OWN RESEARCH CONTRADICTS PART OF OUR PLAN

**Pasa runs the Soil Health Benchmark Study — 100+ farms in PA and Maryland since
2016**, with the Cornell Soil Health Laboratory, Penn State Extension, Rodale
Institute and Stroud Water Research Center. Pasa calls it *"the largest and most
diverse community soil science project in the nation."*

**Luka works for the organisation that produced this. He will know it. We should
too.**

## What they found, verbatim

> *"Our study's **most remarkable revelation challenges a popular theory** among
> farmers and other industry professionals positing that **eliminating tillage is
> always necessary** for achieving optimal soil."*

> *"farms that **rely on tillage** for controlling weeds and preparing fields were
> **also capable of achieving optimal soil health**... by balancing tillage with a
> holistic soil health management strategy"*

> *"organic no-till... **remains largely elusive to most organic farmers** who
> typically depend on at least some 'steel in the field'"*

## 🔴 What this means for us

**Do not walk in selling no-till purity.** Pasa's own data says it is elusive for
organic growers and that tillage, well-timed, is compatible with excellent soil.

⭐ **Reframe, and it becomes stronger, not weaker:** we are not claiming to
eliminate tillage. We are doing **the holistic strategy their data actually
validates** — cover crops, rotation, calibrated amendments, carefully timed
tillage — and **testing whether occultation can replace some of it.**

⭐⭐ **And that is the Adaptive Management hypothesis, handed to us by their own
research:**

> *"Pasa's study found organic no-till largely elusive. We want to test whether
> occultation can replace tillage for bed turnover on intensive salad production."*

**That addresses a gap Pasa itself identified.** It is the single best trial
proposal we could put forward.

# 4. OCCULTATION / TARPING — the only on-farm trial data that exists

**CAFF On-Farm Occultation Research Trial, July 2020**, funded by Western SARE.
**Five small-scale farms, 2019 season.** California, not PA — but it is the only
replicated on-farm occultation dataset I could find anywhere.

## Method — worth copying for our own Adaptive Management trial

> *"beds of the same length were tarped... and compared to a non-tarped control...
> irrigation to pre-germinate weeds in the occultation beds... covered with a 5–7
> mm black tarp (**silage tarps had white side facing down**), and left undisturbed
> for **4 weeks**... After 4 weeks, farmers removed tarps and direct seeded with
> carrots or beets."*

Data collected: labour hours, weed counts in 10"×10" quadrats at 30/60/90% crop
maturity, yield and quality.

## Results — labour hours, control vs occultation

| Farm | Control | Occultation | **Reduction** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kern Family Farm | 16.6 | 11.6 | **−35%** |
| **Super Tuber Farm*** | 6.0 | 3.0 | **−66%** |
| **Mountain Bounty Farm*** | 3.2 | 1.2 | **−91%** |
| First Rain Farm | 1.6 | 1.0 | **−46%** |
| Hillview Farm | 6.7 | 6.4 | −5% |

*\* statistically significant*

> *"For the majority of farms there was a **significant reduction in labour hours**
> in the occultated beds compared to the control."*

⚠️ **Note the honest spread.** Two farms significant, one at −5%. **Occultation is
not uniformly transformative** — which is exactly why a three-year trial on our
own ground is the right thing to be paid for.

⭐ **Two practical details worth taking:** white side down, and four weeks.

# 6. VERMICOMPOST — officially recognised, with one hard constraint

**NOP Guidance 5021** is titled *Compost and Vermicompost in Organic Crop
Production.*

> *"**Vermicompost is an alternative method for meeting the NOP compost
> requirements.**"*

**Methods NOP names:** outdoor windrows (6–12 months) · **angled wedge systems
(2–4 months)** · indoor container systems (2–4 months) · **continuous flow
reactors (30–60 days)**.

🔴 **The constraint:** vermicomposting must stay **below 35°C / 95°F or the worms
die.** CPS 317 specifies **131–170°F** for pathogen kill. **Those are mutually
exclusive.**

⭐ **The way through, from NOP 5021 itself:**

> *"Compost and vermicompost made **without animal materials** as feedstock are
> **not restricted in use**."*

**Plant feedstock only → no 90/120-day restriction → usable on salad greens.**
Manure-fed vermicompost would be restricted and useless on our main crop.
