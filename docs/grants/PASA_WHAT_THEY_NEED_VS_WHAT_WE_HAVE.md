# 3. THE RULES — confirmed from the official PDF

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Spending ceiling | **$15,000 — "soft"**, not hard |
| Per-item limit | **No single item or bulk purchase over $10,000** |
| Equipment exclusion | over $10k **including taxes and fees** = "equipment", not fundable here |
| ⭐ **Shipping** | **"can be considered a separate expense"** — freight does **not** count toward the $10k |
| FSA land | must tie to a **specific FSA-registered field** |
| Two crops | **"Both commodities can be Mixed Vegetables for example"** — looser than feared |
| Conservation practice | must be an **ACTIVE** practice on those crops and that field |
| Soil disturbance | → environmental review → **cannot be funded through BD** |
| Not covered | utilities, insurance, credit card fees, general operating expenses |
| **Double dipping** | *"another **AMP grant or federal program**"* — **AIG and FVPG are STATE, so no conflict** |
