# 2. WHAT THE TWO PLASTICS ACTUALLY DO

| | **Clear — solarization** | **Black / silage — occultation** |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | traps heat, cooks the top few inches | blocks light; weeds germinate, then starve |
| Needs | **strong sun and heat** | works in **cool and cloudy** weather too |
| Season | mid-summer | spring, fall, shoulder seasons |
| Also kills | weed seed, some soilborne pathogens | established weeds, cover crop residue |
| Risk | can cook soil biology if left too long in peak heat | slower |

**Both are worth having, for different windows** — which is exactly what Todd
asked for. Clear for the July–August turnarounds, black for everything else.

# 4. ⚠️ THE 6-FOOT WIDTH IS A SOURCING PROBLEM

**Silage tarp is not sold in 6' widths.** Every verified product above comes in
**30', 32', or 50' wide** sheets. A 32' × 150' sheet cut into 6' strips yields
five strips **150 feet long — not 400.** Getting 6' × 400' means **seaming, or a
custom order.**

## Worth questioning the 6' format itself

| | 6 strips of 6' × 400' | 2 wide sheets over a block of beds |
|---|---|---|
| Pieces to handle | 6 | 2 |
| **Edges to weight down** | **24 long edges** | 8 |
| Wind failure risk | 🔴 high — **edges are where tarps fail** | lower |
| Deploys with a reel | ✅ yes | awkward |
| Per-bed flexibility | ✅ tarp one bed at a time | all or nothing |

**Most market farms occult in blocks** — one wide sheet over four or five beds —
precisely because edges are the failure point and the labor is in weighting them.

➡️ **This is Todd's call, not mine.** Per-bed strips buy flexibility and suit the
reel; block sheets buy fewer edges and less wind risk. **But the 6' × 400' size
has to be custom-ordered or seamed either way**, so it is worth deciding
deliberately rather than by default.

# 5. THE REEL — the right instinct, not yet sourced

A tarp reel is the difference between occultation being routine and being a job
nobody wants. Rolling a wet 2,400 sq ft tarp by hand is miserable, and a tarp put
away badly is a tarp that fails early.

⚠️ **I could not find a verified market-farm tarp winder in this session.** What
turned up was dump-truck tarp rollers, which are the wrong tool.

**Where to look next:**

- **Nolt's** (PA, already an AIG R2 vendor)
- **Market Farm Implement** (PA, already an AIG R2 vendor)

  is already building a tri-clamp manifold and forced-air coolers. **A 3-point or
  skid-mounted reel is well within that.** Labor is grant-eligible at $25/hr.
