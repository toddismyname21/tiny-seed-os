# 3. ⭐⭐ 472 ACCESS CONTROL IS A BETTER FIT FOR DEER THAN 382 EVER WAS

Read CPS 472 directly (`docs/grants/amp_program_docs/NRCS_472_Access_Control.pdf`):

> **Definition:** *"The temporary or permanent **exclusion of animals**, people,
> vehicles, or equipment from an area."*

> **Purposes:** *"**Protect plant productivity and health.** Protect soil from
> compaction. Reduce erosion. Reduce water quality degradation."*

> *"applies on all land uses where **sensitive areas need protection from
> animals**"*

**Deer are animals. Salad greens are plant productivity. Excluding deer from
cropland is literally the definition of the practice.**

⚠️ **One line to be careful of:** *"This practice is not used to manage grazing
and browsing livestock."* **Deer are wildlife, not livestock** — so this exclusion
should not bite. But it is the sentence someone could misread, so have the
distinction ready.

**The standard also asks for:** *"placement, location, dimensions, and materials...
and frequency of use for each activity including monitoring frequency"* and
*"identify animals, people, vehicles, and equipment to be managed."*

➡️ **So the deliverable is a fence plan with a map, dimensions, materials, and a
monitoring schedule — plus a quote.**
