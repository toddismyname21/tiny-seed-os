Built 2026-10-02 against the official **Business Development Overview** PDF and
Chris Esposito's 10/01 email. Every line maps to one of Pasa's own categories.

> **Project:** *Seed to Sale with Salad — Closing the Post-Harvest Gap*

## The frame

AIG (a **state** program) bought the front half of seed-to-sale: precision
seeder, stone burier, cultivation. It stops at harvest. **Everything between
harvest and sale is unfunded** — and that is where the farm loses quality,
labor hours, and access to buyers who need consistent pack weights and shelf
life.

dry it without a queue, cool it fast, and pack it to a consistent, labelled,
traceable weight.

# THE PLAN

## PRIORITY 1 — Bagging. The named bottleneck. ~$6,800

**Today:** greens are dumped into a 200-gallon stock tank and bagged in **three
separate touches — fill, tie, sticker.** Every bag, three times. While that
runs, the greens sit in the tank losing shelf life.

| # | Item | Category | Est. | Verified? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Pack table with digital scales** — fill-to-weight over a funnel, 2 stations | *Handling and Measurements — weight scales* | **$3,800** | quote out |
| 1.2 | **Band sealer with hot-stamp code printer** — replaces hand-tying; prints lot code + pack date as it seals | *Wash/Pack Station infrastructure* | **$2,500** | quote out |
| 1.3 | **Label applicator** | *Packaging Design — product labels* | **$500** | estimate |

**Business improvement:** three touches become one and a half. Pack weights
become consistent, which is the thing wholesale buyers reject you over. Lot code
and pack date are printed by the machine, which is a **GAP traceability
requirement satisfied by equipment rather than a binder.** And every minute saved
is a minute greens are not warming in the tank.

## PRIORITY 2 — Drying. ~$3,500

| # | Item | Category | Est. | Verified? |

| 2.1 | **Veg dryer / spin dryer, 20–40 lb per load** | *Washing and Drying tools — greens washer and dryers* | **$3,500** | quote out |

## PRIORITY 3 — Cooling. ~$1,200

| # | Item | Category | Est. | Verified? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | **Forced-air cooling** — fans, baffle, ducting into the coolers we already own | *Climate Control — coolbots, air conditioners, insulation* | **$900** | estimate |
| 3.2 | **Thermometers / data loggers** for the cooler and the pack line | *Environment Monitoring — sensors and monitors* | **$300** | estimate |

**We are not asking for a cooler — we have coolers.** We are asking to make them
work. A walk-in *holds* temperature; it pulls field heat out of a packed tote
over hours. Forced air pulls cold air *through* the pallet and does it in
minutes. **The first hours after harvest set shelf life**, and shelf life is the
entire wholesale argument. Monitoring proves it to a buyer.
