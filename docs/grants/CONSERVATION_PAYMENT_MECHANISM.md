# 3. Why this is genuinely uncertain, not just unlooked-up

Conservation programs pay in several different shapes, and which one applies
changes everything about how to plan:

| Model | How it works | What it would mean here |
|---|---|---|
| **Per-acre practice payment** | a published rate × acres enrolled, e.g. NRCS pays $X/ac for Mulching 484 | Scale matters. 44 acres of HEL cropland would drive the number |
| **Cost-share percentage** | program pays e.g. 75% of actual cost | Receipts matter. Todd fronts the rest |
| **Flat per-practice** | a set amount per practice adopted | Breadth matters — adopt more practices, not more acres |
| **Advance, like BD's 80/20** | money before the work | No cash fronting |
| **Reimbursement, like AIG** | receipts first, money in ~8 weeks | Todd fronts everything, as he already does on $58,961 of AIG receipts |

**The NRCS payment scenarios I pulled are per-acre and per-unit rates** — that is
how NRCS does it. **Pasa may or may not mirror that.** I used those figures in
the conservation report precisely because they show scope and scale, and I
labelled them as EQIP rates rather than AMP rates for this reason.

# 5. 🔴 THE QUESTIONS FOR LUKA — ask these at the conservation meeting

Ordered so the first three settle the tarps.

1. **How does Conservation Implementation pay — per acre, per practice, flat, or
   a cost-share percentage of actual cost?**
2. **Is it an advance or a reimbursement?** The BD side is 80% on contract
   signing; does Conservation follow the same schedule, or do we front the money
   and submit receipts like AIG?
3. **If reimbursement — how long after receipts?** AIG runs about eight weeks and
   Todd is already carrying $58,961 of AIG receipts.
4. **Is there a cap per producer, per practice, or per acre?**
5. **Does tarping qualify as "Non-Mechanical" under Reduced Till 345, or does it
   go to Mulching 484 Synthetic Material — or both?** They pay very differently.
6. **Does our own labor count, and is it paid or only match?** The BD form pays
   $50/hr owner and $25/hr staff; does Conservation do the same?
7. **We lease all our land. What landlord consent or lease term is required?**
8. **What has to be in place before the clock starts** — a signed GT&C, an
   approved conservation plan, or both?
9. **Can we buy tarps now and have them count, or must the plan be approved
   first?** ⬅️ **this is the practical one**
