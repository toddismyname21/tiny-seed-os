# QUICK START GUIDE
## Get Started with Tiny Seed OS in 5 Minutes

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-03

---

## Step 1: Open Your App (30 seconds)

### Find Your URL

| Your Role | Open This URL |
|-----------|---------------|

---

## Step 2: Log In (30 seconds)

1. Enter your **username** (lowercase, no spaces)
2. Enter your **4-digit PIN**
3. Tap **Sign In**

**First time?** Ask your manager for your username and PIN.

---

## Step 3: Install on Your Phone (1 minute)

### iPhone
1. Open the URL in **Safari**
2. Tap **Share** button (square with arrow)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

### Android
1. Open the URL in **Chrome**
2. Tap **Menu** (three dots)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

Now you have an app icon on your home screen!

---

## Step 4: Do Your First Task (2 minutes)

### For Employees

1. **Clock In**
   - Make sure you're at the farm
   - Tap the big **Clock In** button

2. **View Tasks**
   - Tap **Tasks** tab
   - See your work for today
   - Tasks with red badges are most urgent

3. **Complete a Task**
   - Tap a task
   - Do the work
   - Tap **Complete**
   - Done!

### For Managers

1. **Check Dashboard**
   - See AI-sorted priority queue
   - Red items need immediate attention

2. **Check Team Workload**
   - Look at capacity bars
   - Red = overloaded, needs rebalancing

3. **Review Alerts**
   - AI proactive alerts show predicted issues
   - Take action or dismiss

### For Drivers

1. **View Route**
   - See all stops for today
   - Follow the order shown

2. **Make Delivery**
   - Tap the stop
   - Tap **Navigate** for directions
   - Take photo, get signature
   - Tap **Complete**

## Step 5: Understand Priority (1 minute)

### Priority Badges Tell You What's Most Important

| Badge | Score | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Red** | 80-100 | Do NOW |
| **Orange** | 50-79 | Do today |
| **Yellow** | 30-49 | Important |
| **Green** | 0-29 | Flexible |

### At-Risk Warnings (Yellow Triangle)

If you see a yellow warning, that task has a problem:
- **TIME**: Not enough hours available
- **WEATHER**: Bad weather coming
- **OVERRIPE**: Harvest before quality drops
- **OVERDUE**: Past due date
- **DEPENDENCY**: Waiting on other tasks

## Common First Questions

**Q: I forgot my PIN**
A: Ask your manager or Admin to reset it

**Q: Can't clock in - says "location required"**
A: Make sure GPS is on and you're at the farm

**Q: Task list is empty**
A: You may not have tasks assigned yet - check with your supervisor

**Q: App is slow**
A: Try refreshing the page or check your internet connection
