## Key Business Insights

### 1. CSA is the CORE Business
- CSA subscriptions represent 60-70% of revenue
- Growth is strong: 2025 pre-sales for 2026 season are already at $136K
- Product diversification working: Multiple share sizes and frequencies

### 2. Add-On Program is a Success
- Started from $0 in 2023
- Now at $10K+ in 2025
- Mushrooms most popular, followed by Bread and Cheese
- Coffee add-on is new (2025)

### 3. Flower Business is Evolving
- Standard subscriptions declining
- New tiered model (Full Bloom, Petite Bloom) growing
- Dahlia CSA may be phased out (no 2025 sales)

### 4. Farmers Market Strategy Changing
- Farm Fresh Bouquets down significantly in 2025
- Individual produce sales UP (now tracking via POS)
- Pick Your Own experience growing

### 5. Missing Data Note
- The "blank" rows at top (2023: $81,757 etc.) are TOTALS that don't match the sum of individual products
- This suggests some sales categories are not itemized in this report
- Consider pulling additional reports for complete picture
