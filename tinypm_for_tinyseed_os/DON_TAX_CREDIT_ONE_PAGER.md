# Pennsylvania Beginning Farmer Tax Credit
## How Don Saves $6,300 by Signing the Lease

---

## The Opportunity

Pennsylvania offers landowners a **state income tax credit** when they rent agricultural assets to certified Beginning Farmers.

**You qualify. Todd qualifies. The only missing piece is a written lease.**

## How It Works

1. **Sign the lease** (attached Letter of Understanding)
2. **Todd gets certified** as a Beginning Farmer through PA DCED
3. **You apply** for the tax credit, referencing Todd's certification
4. **You claim the credit** on your PA state tax return

---

## Program Details

- **Created by:** Act 65 of 2019 (Senate Bill 478)
- **Administered by:** PA Dept. of Community & Economic Development (DCED)
- **Available through:** 2030 (10-year program)
- **Annual state allocation:** $6 million (first-come, first-served)
- **Contact:** 1-800-379-7448

---

## What Qualifies

The credit applies to rental of:
- ✅ Land
- ✅ Buildings (barns, greenhouses, coolers)
- ✅ Equipment (tractors, implements)

**Your entire rental arrangement with Todd qualifies.**
