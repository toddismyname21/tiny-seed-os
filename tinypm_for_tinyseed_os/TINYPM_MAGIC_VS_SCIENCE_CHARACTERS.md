# TinyPM Character Family: MAGIC VS SCIENCE

## The Grand Vision

TinyPM exists at the nexus where **ancient wisdom meets cutting-edge innovation**. In this world, spells are algorithms and experiments are incantations. The characters who inhabit this realm understand that the greatest breakthroughs happen when you stop asking "magic OR science?" and start asking "magic AND science?"

---

## THE MAGIC SIDE

**Name:** Archimedes Threadweaver
**Title:** Grand Orchestrator of the Infinite Backlog

He doesn't manage tasks—he weaves destinies. Every sprint is a spell, every roadmap a prophecy.

**Visual:** Midnight-blue robes with constellation patterns, staff topped with spinning crystalline orb, silver beard with glowing runes, half-moon spectacles

**Colors:** Deep midnight blue (#1a1a3e), Burnished gold (#d4a84b), Soft purple glow (#9b6dff)

**Quote:** *"Every task completed is a spell cast into the future. Choose wisely what world you conjure."*

**Colors:** Electric blue (#00d9ff), Clinical white (#f8f9fa), Warning orange (#ff6b35), Tesla purple (#9d4edd)
