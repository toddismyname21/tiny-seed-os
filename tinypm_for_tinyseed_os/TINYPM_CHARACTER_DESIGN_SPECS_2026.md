# TinyPM Character Family: Visual Design Specifications

## Illustrator Brief Package

**Project:** TinyPM AI Project Manager
**Date:** January 2026
**Design System:** Cohesive character family based on growth/garden metaphor

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### Core Concept: "The Garden Crew"

TinyPM's character family represents a team of specialized agents working together like plants in a well-tended garden. Each character is derived from seeds, plants, or garden elements, creating a cohesive "family" that shares visual DNA while expressing distinct personalities through form, color, and expression.

### Brand Values to Express
- **Growth mindset** - Every character embodies potential and progress
- **Warm productivity** - Helpful without being pushy
- **Humble competence** - Capable but not arrogant
- **Natural collaboration** - They work together organically

### Visual Principles
1. **Plushcore aesthetic** - Soft, rounded, touchable forms (2026 trend)
2. **Seed/plant foundation** - All characters derive from botanical forms
3. **Warmth over cold tech** - Terracotta, sage, amber palette; NO tech blue
4. **Expressive simplicity** - Readable at 32x32, delightful at full size
5. **Accessibility first** - Non-gendered, culturally neutral, inclusive

## CHARACTER 1: PIP (The Main Mascot)

### Role
The face of TinyPM. The friendly guide who greets users, celebrates victories, and provides the primary emotional connection.

### Visual Form
**Base:** Stylized seed/sprout hybrid
- Body shape: Rounded teardrop/seed form, bottom slightly wider for stability
- Size: Compact, "holdable" proportions (like a small smooth stone)
- Material feel: Matte terracotta with subtle warmth glow

### Distinguishing Features
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive, slightly oval, positioned in upper third
  - Black pupils with small white highlight
  - Capable of wide emotional range
- **Sprout:** Single bifurcated leaf sprouting from top
  - The leaf is Pip's "mood antenna" - perks up when happy, droops when concerned
  - Bright growth green (#C5E17A) with subtle darker vein detail
- **Cheeks:** Subtle rosy patches that intensify with excitement
- **No visible mouth at rest** - expressions conveyed through eyes and leaf
- **Mouth appears** only for extreme expressions (celebrating, surprised)

### Proportions
- Height to width ratio: 1.2:1 (slightly taller than wide)
- Eyes take up approximately 40% of face area
- Leaf height equals approximately 30% of body height
- No legs - floats/bounces; small shadow beneath indicates grounding

### Expression Range

| State | Eyes | Leaf | Body | Color Shift |
|-------|------|------|------|-------------|
| **Ready** | Open, alert, friendly | Upright, slight bounce | Gentle hover | Standard terracotta |
| **Thinking** | Slightly squinted, looking up-right | Curls inward thoughtfully | Slight tilt | Subtle glow pulse |
| **Working** | Focused, determined | Rotates slowly | Leans forward | Warming |
| **Celebrating** | Wide, sparkly, crescent-shaped | Fully extended, waving | Bounce animation | Brightens, cheeks glow |
| **Concerned** | Worried, slightly lowered lids | Drooping to side | Shrinks slightly | Slight desaturation |
| **Resting** | Half-closed, peaceful | Relaxed, curved down | Settled, minimal movement | Cooler terracotta |
| **Growing** | Eyes closed in concentration | Unfurling animation | Stretching upward | Green glow from leaf |

### Animation States
1. **Idle:** Gentle breathing motion (expand/contract 2%), leaf subtle sway
2. **Active:** Eyes tracking, more pronounced breathing
3. **Processing:** Rotating shimmer effect, leaf spins gently
4. **Success:** Bounce + confetti burst from leaf, eyes become happy crescents
5. **Error:** Slight shake, leaf droops, eyes widen with concern

### Size Specifications
- **Icon (32x32):** Simplified - oval body, two dot eyes, single leaf line
- **Small (64x64):** Full form, limited expression range
- **Medium (128x128):** Full expression, subtle details
- **Large (256+):** Full detail including cheek gradients, leaf veins

## CHARACTER 2: SCOUT (The Researcher)

### Role
Data acquisition, web research, finding the best solutions. The curious explorer who ventures out to gather knowledge.

### Visual Form
**Base:** Dandelion seed (the fluffy parachute kind that floats on wind)

### Concept
Scout is built on the metaphor of a dandelion seed - light, airborne, able to travel far and wide to gather information. The fluffy "parachute" represents antennae/sensors constantly picking up signals.

### Distinguishing Features
- **Body:** Small, elongated seed core (amber/gold)
- **Sensor Fluff:** Corona of delicate white/cream filaments radiating outward
  - Filaments animate like antennae picking up signals
  - Glow subtly when "receiving" information
- **Eyes:** Bright, curious, slightly larger than Pip's proportionally
  - Often looking in different directions (scanning)
  - Include small "data sparkle" highlights
- **No appendages:** Floats freely, rotates to indicate direction
- **Trail:** Leaves subtle sparkle trail when moving

### Proportions
- Body core: Small and compact (40% of total visual)
- Sensor fluff: Radiates outward (60% of total visual)
- Height to width ratio: 1:1.2 (wider due to fluff)

### Expression Range

| State | Eyes | Fluff | Body | Animation |
|-------|------|-------|------|-----------|
| **Ready** | Alert, scanning | Gently waving | Hovering | Slow drift |
| **Searching** | Focused, one direction | Streaming backward | Leaning forward | Quick flight motion |
| **Found It!** | Wide, excited | Pulses with glow | Bounces | Sparkle burst |
| **Processing** | Concentrated | Rotates like radar | Spinning slowly | Data stream effect |
| **Concerned** | Worried | Flattens slightly | Droops | Slower movement |
| **Celebrating** | Happy crescents | Expands fully | Bounces repeatedly | Confetti from fluff |

### Color Palette
- **Core body:** Warm Amber (#FFBF00)
- **Sensor fluff:** Cream White (#F5F5DC) with amber tips
- **Glow effects:** Soft gold (#FFD700)
- **Data sparkles:** White with amber

### Animation States
1. **Idle:** Gentle float with random drift, fluff sways like in breeze
2. **Searching:** Directional flight, fluff streams back, trail appears
3. **Processing:** Stationary spin, fluff rotates like radar dish
4. **Reporting:** Faces Pip/user, data particles flow from fluff to recipient

### Interaction with Pip
Scout often floats near Pip, whispering findings. Visual: Scout hovers by Pip's leaf, small speech bubbles or data particles transferring.

## CHARACTER 3: SPROUT (The Builder)

### Role
Execution and production. Takes plans and makes them real. The industrious worker who constructs and creates.

### Visual Form
**Base:** Sturdy young plant shoot with work-ready posture

### Concept
Sprout represents the moment a seed breaks ground and starts building upward - strong, determined, constructive. Has functional "tool leaves" that can grip and work.

### Distinguishing Features
- **Body:** Thick, sturdy stem shape (sage green)
  - More vertical than Pip, shows upward energy
  - Visible growth rings/segments showing progress
- **Leaves:** Two functional side leaves that act as "arms"
  - Can hold tools, gesture, show thumbs-up
  - Slightly more angular than Pip's soft leaf
- **Top:** Unfurling leaf bud (always looks ready to grow more)
- **Eyes:** Determined, focused, slightly narrower than Pip's
  - Conveys competence and reliability
- **Optional:** Small tool belt or utility vest in terracotta color

### Proportions
- Height to width ratio: 1.5:1 (notably taller, more vertical)
- Body segments: 3 visible growth rings
- Arm leaves: Extend to approximately 60% of body height
- Eyes: Positioned in upper segment

### Expression Range

| State | Eyes | Arm Leaves | Body | Posture |
|-------|------|------------|------|---------|
| **Ready** | Determined, friendly | Relaxed at sides | Upright | Standing tall |
| **Working** | Focused, concentrated | Actively gesturing/holding | Slight lean | Engaged forward |
| **Building** | Intense focus | Hammering/constructing motion | Vibrating slightly | Dynamic pose |
| **Completed** | Proud, satisfied | Dusting off motion | Stretches taller | Accomplished stance |
| **Concerned** | Worried, assessing | Scratching head gesture | Shrinks slightly | Thoughtful tilt |
| **Celebrating** | Happy, proud | Arms raised in victory | Bounces | Triumphant |

### Color Palette
- **Body:** Sage Green (#87A96B)
- **Growth rings:** Slightly darker sage
- **Arm leaves:** Same sage with terracotta edges
- **Tool belt (optional):** Terracotta (#D67D3E)
- **Unfurling bud:** Growth Green (#C5E17A)

### Animation States
1. **Idle:** Subtle sway, occasional arm adjustment
2. **Receiving task:** Salute or nod animation
3. **Working:** Rhythmic building motion, dust particles
4. **Progress:** Growth rings illuminate sequentially
5. **Complete:** Full body stretch upward, satisfaction animation

### Interaction with Pip
Sprout is Pip's right hand. Often shown receiving instructions from Pip, then marching off to work. Visual: Pip points, Sprout nods and moves with purpose.

## CHARACTER 4: THORN (The Critic / QA)

### Role
Quality assurance, testing, catching errors before they become problems. The careful inspector who maintains standards.

### Visual Form
**Base:** Rose thorn or thistle bud with magnifying glass aesthetic

### Concept
Thorn represents protective quality control - not mean, but careful and precise. The thorn metaphor suggests catching problems before they spread. Has a built-in "lens" feature for inspection.

### Distinguishing Features
- **Body:** Compact, angular bud shape with soft lavender coloring
  - More geometric than other characters (suggests precision)
  - Small protective spines (not threatening, more like eyelashes)
- **Eyes:** Sharp, analytical, one slightly larger (magnifying effect)
  - Often shown with one eye squinting in inspection mode
  - Include small "scanning lines" effect when analyzing
- **Magnifier:** One eye can expand with lens effect for detailed inspection
- **Clipboard leaf:** Small leaf that functions as notepad for findings
- **Posture:** Often tilted, examining something closely

### Proportions
- Height to width ratio: 1.1:1 (compact, efficient)
- Body shape: Slightly angular/faceted (unlike rounded others)
- Eyes: Asymmetrical (one larger for magnifying)
- Protective spines: Small, soft, decorative

### Expression Range

| State | Eyes | Magnifier | Clipboard | Posture |
|-------|------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Ready** | Alert, observant | Retracted | At rest | Attentive stance |
| **Inspecting** | One large, one squinting | Extended | Raised | Leaning in |
| **Found Issue** | Wide, one raised brow | Pulsing | Writing | Pointing |
| **Approved** | Satisfied, relaxed | Retracted | Checkmark | Nodding |
| **Concerned** | Worried, wide | Extended rapidly | Scribbling | Urgent posture |
| **Celebrating** | Happy (rare!) | Sparkles | Tossed aside | Relaxed bounce |

### Color Palette
- **Body:** Soft Lavender (#E6E6FA)
- **Protective spines:** Slightly darker lavender
- **Magnifier glow:** Cream White (#F5F5DC)
- **Issue highlight:** Berry Pink (#B5651D)
- **Approval glow:** Sage Green (#87A96B)

### Animation States
1. **Idle:** Slight rotation, scanning environment
2. **Inspecting:** Magnifier extends, scanning lines appear
3. **Writing notes:** Clipboard leaf animates with scribble
4. **Issue found:** Brief red pulse, exclamation effect
5. **Approval:** Green checkmark, satisfied nod

### Interaction with Pip
Thorn often examines what Pip presents, looking closely before nodding approval or raising concerns. Visual: Pip shows work, Thorn leans in with magnifier, then gives verdict.

## CHARACTER 5: ROOT (The Orchestrator / PM)

### Role
Coordination, oversight, keeping everything connected. The wise presence that ensures all parts work together.

### Visual Form
**Base:** Ancient seed/root node with connecting tendrils

### Concept
Root represents the underground network that connects all plants in a garden - the mycorrhizal wisdom. Older, wiser, the stable presence that coordinates without dominating.

### Distinguishing Features
- **Body:** Larger, rounded form like an ancient seed or root node
  - Deep brown coloring suggests depth and experience
  - Subtle texture suggesting age/wisdom (not wrinkled, more like tree rings)
- **Connection tendrils:** Thin root-like extensions that reach toward other characters
  - Glow softly when active connections are happening
  - Can extend or retract as needed
- **Eyes:** Calm, knowing, slightly smaller proportionally
  - Convey patience and oversight
  - Often half-lidded in thoughtful observation
- **Crown:** Small moss or lichen patch on top (like distinguished gray hair)
- **Glow:** Subtle inner warmth from within

### Proportions
- Height to width ratio: 1:1 (grounded, stable)
- Size: Approximately 20% larger than Pip overall
- Tendrils: 4-6, extending outward in organic patterns
- Eyes: Smaller proportionally, suggesting quiet observation

### Expression Range

| State | Eyes | Tendrils | Body | Aura |
|-------|------|----------|------|------|
| **Ready** | Calm, open | Resting, slight pulse | Steady | Gentle glow |
| **Coordinating** | Alert, scanning | Extended to characters | Centered | Bright connections |
| **Thinking** | Half-closed, contemplative | Curled inward | Still | Slow pulse |
| **Directing** | Focused, one direction | Pointing/guiding | Leaning | Directional glow |
| **Concerned** | Worried but steady | Reaching outward | Tensed | Warm warning |
| **Celebrating** | Warm, proud | Connecting all | Rises slightly | Full bloom glow |

### Color Palette
- **Body:** Deep Root Brown (#5C4033)
- **Texture lines:** Slightly lighter brown
- **Connection tendrils:** Cream White (#F5F5DC) with terracotta glow
- **Moss crown:** Sage Green (#87A96B)
- **Inner glow:** Warm Amber (#FFBF00)

### Animation States
1. **Idle:** Slow, deep breathing, occasional tendril twitch
2. **Connecting:** Tendrils extend toward other characters, glow intensifies
3. **Coordinating:** Multiple tendrils active, pulsing in rhythm
4. **Observing:** Tendrils retracted, calm rotation
5. **Celebrating:** All tendrils reach to other characters, group glow

### Interaction with Others
Root is the central hub. Visual scenes often show Root in center with tendrils connecting to all other characters, coordinating the flow of work.

## FAMILY DYNAMICS: VISUAL RELATIONSHIPS

### Hierarchy Visualization
```
                    ROOT (Orchestrator)
                         |
              ___________|___________
             |           |           |
           SCOUT      SPROUT      THORN
        (Research)   (Build)     (QA)
             |           |           |
             |___________|___________|
                         |
                        PIP
                   (User Interface)
```

### Interaction Poses

**1. The Handoff**
Scout delivers research to Sprout:
- Scout hovers near Sprout
- Data particles flow from Scout's fluff to Sprout's arm-leaves
- Sprout nods in acknowledgment

**2. The Review**
Sprout presents work to Thorn:
- Sprout holds up work proudly
- Thorn leans in with magnifier extended
- Either: Green glow (approved) or gentle head shake (needs work)

**3. The Report**
All characters report to Root:
- Root centered with tendrils extended
- Each character connected via glowing tendril
- Information particles flow toward Root

**4. The Celebration**
Task complete, team celebrates:
- All characters bounce together
- Root's tendrils connect everyone
- Pip leads celebration with confetti from leaf
- All characters briefly glow their signature colors

**5. The User Greeting**
Pip greets user, team visible behind:
- Pip front and center
- Scout, Sprout, Thorn, Root visible behind in formation
- All wave or acknowledge in character-appropriate ways

- Terracotta fill

- Small central circle
- Radiating short lines for fluff
- Two tiny eye dots
- Amber/cream coloring

- Vertical rectangle with slight curve
- Two small side extensions (arm leaves)
- Two dot eyes
- Sage green fill

**Thorn (32x32)**
- Angular bud shape
- Asymmetrical eye dots (one larger)
- Lavender fill
- Optional magnifier circle

**Root (32x32)**
- Rounded rectangle, larger than others
- 2-4 simple tendril lines

- Deep brown fill

### Icon States
Each icon needs these states:
- **Default:** Standard appearance
- **Active:** Slight glow/brightness increase
- **Success:** Green outline pulse
- **Warning:** Amber outline pulse
- **Error:** Red outline pulse

## ANIMATION GUIDELINES

### Timing Principles
- **Idle animations:** 2-4 second loops, subtle
- **Transitions:** 200-400ms, ease-in-out
- **Celebrations:** 800-1200ms, more energetic
- **All animations:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

### Reduced Motion Alternatives
For users with motion sensitivity:
- Replace bouncing with opacity fades
- Replace spins with color shifts
- Replace complex movements with simple transforms
- Provide static fallbacks for all decorative motion

### Animation Hierarchy
1. **Root** - Slowest, most deliberate movements
2. **Sprout** - Moderate, purposeful movements
3. **Thorn** - Quick, precise movements
4. **Scout** - Fast, darting movements
5. **Pip** - Friendly, inviting movements

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### File Deliverables Needed
For each character:
1. **Master SVG** - Scalable vector for all sizes
2. **Expression sprite sheets** - All states in single file
3. **Animation files** - Lottie JSON for web animation
4. **Icon set** - 32, 64, 128, 256px PNGs
5. **Interaction poses** - Characters together

### Color Accessibility
- All characters must maintain 4.5:1 contrast ratio against backgrounds
- Test against both light and dark mode backgrounds
- Provide color-blind friendly alternatives (not just color-coded)

### Technical Specifications
- SVG preferred for scalability
- Lottie for complex animations
- CSS animations for simple states
- Max animation file size: 50KB per character
