RE: Beginning Farmer Tax Credit Certification Application - Todd Wilson / Tiny Seed Farm LLC
Dear Beginning Farmer Tax Credit Program Administrator:

I am pleased to submit my application for certification as a Beginning Farmer under the Pennsylvania Beginning Farmer Tax Credit Program established by Act 65 of 2019. I am the owner and operator of Tiny Seed Farm LLC, a USDA Certified Organic diversified vegetable, cut flower, and gourmet mushroom operation located in Beaver County, Pennsylvania.

I established Tiny Seed Farm in 2017 and have been operating it as my primary occupation and sole source of income since that time. The farm has grown from a small market garden to a significant commercial operation serving over 360 CSA member households, wholesale restaurant accounts, and local farmers markets throughout the Pittsburgh region.

My agricultural journey began in May 2011 as an apprentice at Grow Pittsburgh, an urban farming non-profit. From December 2011 through August 2016, I worked full-time at Kretschmann Farm under the mentorship of Don Kretschmann, learning all aspects of organic vegetable production on his established 40+ year organic operation. This nearly 5-year intensive training prepared me to launch my own farm in 2017.

I now lease land and equipment from Kretschmann Farm (owned by Don Kretschmann) at 257 Zeigler Rd, Rochester, PA 15074. I am applying for Beginning Farmer certification so that Mr. Kretschmann may apply for the associated tax credit as the owner of agricultural assets being leased to a certified beginning farmer. We have no ownership or partnership relationship; he is strictly my landlord under a land and equipment lease arrangement.

My first Schedule F (Farm Income) was filed for tax year 2018, meaning I have approximately 7 years of agricultural income history, well within the 10-year threshold for Beginning Farmer eligibility. I provide 100% of the management decisions and the majority of the labor for the farming operation.

Enclosed Application Materials:

  • Form DCED-BFTC-001 - Completed Beginning Farmer Certification Application (2 pages)
  • Appendix A: Pro Forma Schedule F - 2026 Projected Earnings Statement demonstrating profit potential ($394,480 projected gross income)
  • Appendix A: Income Verification - Historical revenue data showing 40%+ year-over-year growth and farming as primary income source
  • Appendix A: Farm Description - Complete description of farming operation, sales channels, and infrastructure

I am committed to continuing my farming operation in Pennsylvania for the foreseeable future. The landowner, Don Kretschmann, intends to place the property into an Agricultural Land Trust, and I plan to continue farming this land in partnership with that conservation effort.
