## The PA Beginning Farmer Tax Credit Program

### Program Overview

Created by Act 65 of 2019 (Senate Bill 478), this program provides Pennsylvania state income tax credits to landowners who **sell or rent** agricultural assets to certified beginning farmers.

### What Qualifies as "Agricultural Assets"

The program covers:
- **Land** (farmland)
- **Buildings** (barns, greenhouses, coolers)
- **Equipment** (tractors, implements, machinery)

**This means Don's ENTIRE rental arrangement qualifies** - not just the land, but the machinery and infrastructure too.

## Requirements for Don to Qualify

### 1. Todd Must Be Certified as a "Beginning Farmer"

**Definition**: Someone who has been farming for **10 years or less**, determined by not having filed a Schedule F (Farm Income) for more than 10 years.

**Todd likely qualifies** if Tiny Seed Farm was established within the last 10 years.

**No age limit** - this is about farming experience, not age.

### 2. Written Lease Agreement Required

The program **requires a written lease agreement**. This is exactly what Todd needs for security.

The lease Don drafted in 2023 (which was never finalized) would satisfy this requirement.

### 3. Application Process

1. Todd applies to be certified as a "Beginning Farmer" through PA DCED
2. Don applies for the tax credit, referencing Todd's certification
3. Credits are awarded on a first-come, first-served basis (up to $6M available annually through 2030)
