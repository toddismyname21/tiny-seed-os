Before sending ANY member email, show Todd the **actual copy** and get his go on the wording — every single email. For a **batch** (e.g. a schedule-change notice to N members), show the **template copy + the recipient list** and wait for approval before sending.

**Why:** Todd: *"I want to know what each email is about before we send it. I can't fly blind."* (2026-06-19, after I batch-sent 7 flower-alignment heads-up emails having only gotten his approval for the underlying ACTION — aligning flowers to veg week — not the email wording.) Approving an action ≠ approving the email that announces it. Everything goes out under the farm's name to real customers; he needs eyes on the words.

**How to apply:** Even when Todd says "go for it" / "do it" / "go ahead and tell her" on a task that includes notifying members, that authorizes the data/ops change — then STILL present the email draft (or batch template + recipients) and wait for an EXPLICIT "send it" on the exact wording before hitting Resend. The only sends that don't need fresh copy-approval are ones where he just reviewed that exact draft. Pairs with [[email-send-discipline]] and the one-at-a-time review cadence.

**REPEAT FAILURE — 2026-06-22 (Nancy Bergman):** Todd said "go ahead and tell her [the box is auto-populated]" and I (1) sent WITHOUT showing the final wording, and (2) the content was factually WRONG — I wrote "your CSA share comes automatically even if you do nothing." FALSE: flex members must **sign in and SUBMIT their order every week** or no box comes (the page only PRE-FILLS a regular share to make it fast — pre-filled ≠ auto-sent). Sent to a struggling, less-tech-savvy customer — exactly who a wrong/confusing email can LOSE. Two hard rules reinforced: (a) NEVER send any customer email without Todd's explicit confirmation of the EXACT final text — "go ahead" is NOT that; (b) VERIFY factual claims before sending, especially flex mechanics (see [[flex-explainer]]: pre-filled default box, member MUST approve/submit by cutoff or it won't send). When a customer is already confused, prefer a HUMAN fix (Todd/Frankie phone call) over another email.

The rule has NO exemptions: **every outbound email under Todd's or the farm's name — members, chefs, partners, state agencies, 1:1 or batch, Resend or Gmail — gets its draft shown, then WAIT for approval of that exact text.** "Go ahead and send the email" = approval to DRAFT and queue, not to transmit. Only send after he has seen the exact final wording.
