**St. Ferdinand's — Gleaner's Food Bank (Linda Leary, 412-585-5183) is billed
DIRECTLY. It is NOT a PASS agency and NOT invoiced through Greater Pittsburgh
Community Food Bank.** QuickBooks customer is `St. Ferdinand's` **#1095**.

**Why:** the portal account carried a note reading "GPCFB PASS program agency…
Invoice to the Food Bank w/ sales order #" — wrong, and Todd corrected it on
2026-08-25. Acting on it would have sent Linda's invoices to Marc Rattay's AP
queue at the Food Bank, where they would never be paid, on top of the two that
already sat unsent for two months.

Do NOT confuse with [[csa-locations]]-adjacent PASS work: **Center for Hope**
IS a real PASS agency billed to the Food Bank (QB #1099) with a Sales Order
number. Two different relationships, similar-looking names.

**How to apply:**
- Invoices are **handed to Linda in person** at delivery. She has **no email on
  file** — do not wait for an emailed invoice to be paid, and do not assume
  unsent = unpaid. Both #10124 ($800) and #7849647 ($952) were paid in person.
- Payments in this book are recorded with **no PaymentMethod and no deposit
  account** (all 31 since June) — QuickBooks lands them in Undeposited Funds.
  Match that pattern.
- Buys **monthly, last Wednesday**. On site **7:00 AM**, loaded by **8:15 AM**.
  Serves ~80 clients.
- She also receives produce from the county and the Food Bank and **cannot take
  duplicates** — always confirm the list with her before harvesting.
- Pricing is her own, not the PASS rate card: tomato flats **$27.50 each**
  (10 lb), green beans **$3.00** per 1 lb bag, sweet peppers **$30.00** per half
  bushel (set 2026-08-25).
- There are still **two portal accounts** for this one customer — `St. Ferdinand`
  (holds the 6/24 salad-mix order) and `St. Ferdinand's — Gleaner's Food Bank`.
  Both point at QB #1095. Merge decision still open with Todd.
