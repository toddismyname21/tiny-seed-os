When explaining a **Farm Flex** share to a member, always use this framing: each week the Flex share comes **PRE-FILLED with the traditional (small) CSA box**. The member has two options — (1) **just APPROVE it as-is** to receive that week's farmer's-choice box, OR (2) **empty the share and pick their own items from scratch** to customize. Either way they **must take that action by the Tuesday 8 AM cutoff** (the list opens Thursday); it won't send without their approval. They can also skip a week (balance just waits — nothing is lost) or shop a market and spend the balance at the stand.

**Why:** Todd's directive 2026-06-18. Saying only "nothing comes automatically, you must order" confused first-time flex members (they thought they had to build a box from scratch every week, or that they'd missed something). The accurate, friendlier model is "your box is already filled with our pick — just approve it, or customize." Flex members repeatedly hit this confusion in the launch-week inbox (Amy Yurkovich, Lois Brown, Nancy Bergman).

**How to apply:** use this in every flex-member reply about ordering/"do I have to order each week." Note flex debits only on order, so a missed week is no loss. Related: [[flex-cutoff]] is Thursday-open / Tuesday-8AM-close. See also project memory on flex portal state.
