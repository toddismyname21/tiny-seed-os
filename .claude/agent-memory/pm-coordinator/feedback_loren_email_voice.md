Weekly bouquet emails in Loren's voice must be **short** (a few sentences of what's-in-it + brief care notes), greet with "Hi everyone," (NOT "Hi flower friends"), never call the bouquet a "bunch", and close simply ("Enjoy the flowers!" — NOT "thanks for supporting the flower field", NOT "see you next week").

**Why:** Loren edited the 2026-09-03 draft down with exactly these corrections, one by one. The earlier long WHAT'S IN THE BUNCH format (8/27) is not her preferred voice.

**How to apply:** Start every bouquet-email draft from the 2026-09-03 sent version (`loren/notes/2026-08-31_weekly_bouquet_email_draft.md`) as the template, not the older long ones baked into send_flower_wk.ts history. Related: [[loren-workspace]].
