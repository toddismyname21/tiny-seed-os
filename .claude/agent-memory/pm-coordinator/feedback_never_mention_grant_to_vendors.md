**Never disclose to a vendor that a purchase is grant-funded.**

**Why:** Todd, 2026-10-01, reviewing equipment quote requests — *"can you leave
off the part about grants? I am concerned we might get gouged if they have that
information."* A seller who knows public money is paying, and knows the budget
ceiling, has every incentive to quote to the ceiling instead of to the market.

**How to apply:** grant programs want written contractor quotes on letterhead
with freight itemized. Ask for exactly that — **without the reason.** It is an
ordinary purchasing request and needs no justification:

> *"If you could send this as a written quote on your letterhead with freight
> listed separately, that would help — I'm comparing a few options and need the
> numbers side by side."*

True, useful, and gives away nothing. The same applies to naming a budget: do
not tell a vendor the farm has $15,000 to spend. Ask what it costs.

Extends to: loan proceeds, insurance settlements, and any other funded purchase
where the seller could price to the pot rather than the product.
