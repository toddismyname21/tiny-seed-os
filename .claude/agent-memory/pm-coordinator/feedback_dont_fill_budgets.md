**Never propose spending because money is unallocated.** Only budget things Todd
has described as an actual problem.

**Why:** On 2026-10-02 he asked me to find gaps in AIG grant coverage. I found
them correctly, then turned the list into $6,598 of recommendations — cooler
racking, vented totes, dollies, pallet jack, stainless surfaces — to fill the
PASA $15,000. He cut all of it: *"DONT need cooler racking or vented bulk
containers, I don't necessarily want to fund any of this."*

Two distinct errors:

1. **Inferring need from absence of funding.** AIG not having paid for racks
   does not mean the farm lacks racks. It has operated for years and owns
   ordinary pack-house hardware. Same shape as inferring a fact from a code
   comment — see [[no-guessing]].
2. **Treating a soft ceiling as a target.** PASA's $15,000 is explicitly soft.
   Unallocated money is a fine end state.

**The tell:** every line Todd kept came from him naming a real problem — bags
take three touches, the PVC manifold isn't food-grade, residue needs cutting in
place. Every line he cut came from me reading a category list and inventing a
need. **If I can't quote Todd describing the problem, the line doesn't belong in
the budget.**

**How to apply:** On any budget, grant, or purchase list — present what the
money could cover only if asked, and keep it clearly separate from what is
recommended. When money is left over, say so and stop. Ask what is actually
slowing the farm down rather than offering a menu. Padding also costs real
money later: in PASA's case 20% is withheld until Project Completion
Verification, so a budgeted-but-unbought item becomes a verification problem.
