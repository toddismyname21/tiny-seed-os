## 🔴 The fact that changes the numbers

**Production is expanding from 7.6 to 10–12 acres next year** (Todd 2026-10-02).

Flat practices (345, 340, 595, 329, 590 = $25,356) do not move with acreage.
Per-acre practices go from ~$12,509 to ~$19,751.

**Why:** at 7.6 acres the flat "Adaptive Management" scenarios dominate and
per-acre barely matters; at 12 acres per-acre becomes worth fighting for.

**How to apply:** **enrol at the planned acreage, not the current one** — a
contract written against 7.6 acres undersells by thousands. Related:
[[amp-conservation-research]], [[hel-cropland]].
