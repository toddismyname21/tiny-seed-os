44.05 HEL acres = 45% of the 97.41 mapped acres, and **100% of the cropland.**
The cropland indicator is set on exactly the four HEL fields and nowhere else.

**Why:** it makes soil erosion the documented, USDA-recognised resource concern
on all cropland, so erosion practices need no justification; conservation
compliance already applies (AD-1026 certified and current); and HEL ground is
typically weighted favourably in conservation ranking.

**How to apply:** lead every conservation funding conversation with this. It is
why Todd's tarping / no-till plan is the textbook response rather than a
preference. Field 1068/446/1 at 33.55 acres is the single biggest target — it is
where contour buffer strips and no-till pay most. Related: [[amp-conservation-research]].
