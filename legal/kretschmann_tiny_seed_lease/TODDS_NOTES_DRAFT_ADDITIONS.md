### NEW NOTE — Place at Article 10 (Remedy for Violation)

**Where:** After Article 10.1 ("work in good faith to resolve any issues")

> [NEW] This is a significant change from the 2023 lease, which included a 14-day written cure period, specific notice requirements, and mediation before termination. I would like to restore that structure. Specifically:
>
