## WHY THIS MATTERS

Under standard agricultural lease practices (and reflected in our proposed lease), repairs fall into two categories:

### CAPITAL REPAIRS (Landlord Responsibility)
- Structural repairs: roofs, foundations, walls, siding
- Major systems: well pumps, septic, electrical panels
- Major equipment repairs: engine overhauls, transmission rebuilds
- Items that extend the useful life of the asset

### ROUTINE MAINTENANCE (Tenant Responsibility)
- Minor repairs: door handles, light bulbs, small fixes
- Regular upkeep: oil changes, greasing, blade sharpening
- Consumables and operational wear items

The distinction is based on the **type** of repair, not just the cost.
