## STANDARD PRACTICE (Per Extension Guidelines)

From the 2023 draft lease: *"To fairly apportion utility and fuel costs, as well as cost or labor of maintenance with cases of multiple users."*

**Standard approaches:**
- **Option A:** Tenant pays percentage of fuel PURCHASES (not existing inventory)
- **Option B:** Tenant pays for fuel they USE (metered or estimated)
- **Option C:** Landlord provides fuel, included in rent

**What appears to have happened:**
- Todd paid for existing fuel inventory (Invoice 788)
- Todd paid percentage shares as if he didn't own that fuel
- Todd paid to replace the fuel

This is not consistent with standard "fair apportionment."
