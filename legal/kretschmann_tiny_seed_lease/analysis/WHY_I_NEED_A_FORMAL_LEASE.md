# WHY I NEED A FORMAL LEASE

## The Core Issue

**I cannot access financing without a formal, signed lease agreement.**

- Banks require it for operating loans
- FSA requires it for beginning farmer programs
- Grant programs require it as proof of land access
- The 2023 draft was never finalized - I have no documentation

---

## The Catch-22

```
I owe Don money from 2024
        ↓
Don wants me to get a loan to pay him
        ↓
Banks/FSA require a formal lease to approve a loan
        ↓
I don't have a formal lease
        ↓
I can't get a loan
        ↓
I can't pay Don
        ↓
(cycle repeats)
```

**A formal lease breaks this cycle.**

---

## What I've Done to Get Here

To keep the farm running in 2024, I:
- Took out loans
- Used a merchant cash advance (high interest)
- Drew from my retirement savings
- Still ended up owing Don money

This is not sustainable. I need proper financing access.

---

## What I'm Asking For

1. **A formal, signed lease** - So I can apply for loans and grants
2. **Sustainable terms** - So I can actually make the payments
3. **Clarification on some charges** - So we both understand the arrangement
4. **Clear fuel policy** - So we don't have confusion about what's owed

---

## The Fuel Confusion (Example of Why We Need Clarity)

The current informal arrangement has led to confusion about fuel charges:

| Payment | Amount | Question |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Invoice 788 (Dec 2024) | $914 | Paid for fuel in tanks |
| June-Oct 2025 shares | $1,207.40 | Charged again for same fuel? |
| Diesel tank refill | $822.78 | Paid 100% but share was 60%? |
| Invoice 812 "Misc" | $135.29 | No description provided |
| **Total** | **$3,079.47** | |

**This isn't about disputing charges - it's about clarity.**

Without a formal lease specifying fuel arrangements:
- Neither of us knows exactly what's owed
- Charges get applied inconsistently
- Corrections have to be made after the fact (like Invoice 832x)
- Both parties are frustrated

**A formal lease with clear fuel terms prevents this confusion.**

---

## What Don Gets

- **Payment of outstanding balance** - Once I have financing
- **A formal legal agreement** - Protecting his property
- **A viable tenant** - Who can pay reliably going forward
- **Continuation of his legacy** - The land stays in active farming

---

## The Alternative

Without a formal lease:
- I remain unable to access financing
- I remain in debt to Don
- Don remains an unwilling "lender"
- The farm operation remains unstable
- Nobody wins

---

**A formal lease is the solution that serves both of our interests.**
