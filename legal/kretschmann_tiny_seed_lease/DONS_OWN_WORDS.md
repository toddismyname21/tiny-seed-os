## LETTER 1: "Getting the Farm Band Back Together"

**From:** Don Kretschmann
**To:** Former CSA members (HUNDREDS of recipients)
**Date:** December 31, 2024 / January 2025
**Subject:** Getting the farm band back together again

> "Holiday Greetings from the Kretschmanns,
>
> As life continues to throw the normal range of challenges our way — no discount for age — we've been blessed over the last two years to have a former employee, Todd, return to lease land from us and continue to produce beautiful organic vegetables.
>
> **In a nutshell, the farm will remain in a land trust and Todd will have a long-term lease where he can continue to grow organic produce indefinitely.**
>
> We can give the highest endorsement to Todd and the quality of the produce he's been growing. In the 10 years since he left us to launch Tiny Seed Farm, he's been supplier to many of the best restaurants in the 'burgh. He's quite adventurous in trying new crops — the variety is simply astonishing.
>
> Todd is interested in expanding his CSA and asked us to contact prior subscribers.
>
> P.S. This year, there's the warm fuzzy feel like getting the band back together, as Todd is recruiting several of our longtime Mexican workers to help him."

**Key commitments in this letter:**
- "Long-term lease" — not 1 year + 2 renewals
- "Indefinitely" — not 3 years maximum
- "Highest endorsement" — Don publicly vouches for Todd
- "Land trust" — farm preserved permanently
- Sent to HUNDREDS of former customers — this is a public promise

## LETTER 2: "A Plea for the Future of Our Farm"

**From:** Don Kretschmann
**To:** Former CSA members
**Date:** September 9, 2024

> "Since we retired after the 2020 season, we've been searching for the right farmer(s) to continue farming this 80 A of Western PA which we've had the privilege of loving and caring for these nearly 50 years. **We're on the path to donating the land itself to a community farmland trust to be leased on a permanent basis to the next generation of farmers.** The good news is that currently we have a former employee, leasing about 5 A and growing a wide variety of organic vegetables, much as we had done for decades. He's planning on expanding production next season.
>
> ...
>
> It goes back to the very meaning of CSA — Community Sponsored (or Supported) Agriculture. You were the reason we could farm profitably and sustainably all these years. We need your support now if our orchard is to survive.
>
> ...
>
> Corporations have farm-washed their produce offerings — 'The Farmers Market at Giant Eagle'. Some have even CSA-washed their businesses. The love of place implicit in locally grown food has been prostituted."

**Key commitments in this letter:**
- "Permanent basis" — not 1-year renewals
- "Next generation of farmers" — Don frames Todd as the future
- "Nearly 50 years" — Don's legacy, which Todd is being asked to continue
- "Community farmland trust" — specific plan, not just aspiration
