## 🔴 ARTICLE 8 — FUEL: as drafted it permits a DOUBLE CHARGE

This is the live dispute. The tank is empty as of 2026-08-20 and there is no agreed
process for refilling it.

**3. 8.2 bills fuel twice.**
- **8.2 main** charges a *monthly* fuel cost on tractor hours (ASABE formula), reconciled
  twice yearly.
- **8.2(c)** *also* splits *refill* cost by the previous six months' usage ratio.

Nothing states that one nets against the other, so the same diesel can be billed on hours
AND again at the pump. Todd's own margin note already anticipates this — *"can use it so
long as it reconciles with the actual fuel use total"* — that reconciliation needs to be
an operative sentence, not a note.

**Ask for ONE mechanism, not both:**
- *Refill-only* — each fill split by the prior six months' usage ratio. Simple,
  self-correcting, needs no hour logs. **Preferred.**
- *Hours-only* — bill hours monthly; whoever buys the fuel is reimbursed from the other's hours.

**4. 8.2(c) — "fuel in tanks belongs to Lessor" should be struck or qualified.**
As written, fuel Todd pays for becomes Don's asset the moment it is pumped. Todd paid Reed
Oil **$822.78** direct (cheque #7136, 28 Oct 2025) for a full refill. Suggested wording:
fuel purchased by a party remains that party's property until consumed, and any unconsumed
balance is credited on termination.

**5. Confirm the October 2025 tank is settled.**
Todd paid $822.78; Don credited **$329.00** ("Don's share · .4 × 823"). That leaves Todd
carrying $493.78 for his ~60%. Don has since said verbally he intends to *also* bill Todd
for usage on that tank — which is the double charge in item 3. **Get the settled position
stated in writing.**

**6. Propane — Todd is bearing 100% on one unanswered sentence.**
Don's 22 Mar 2026 email: *"assumes you used the propane and gas."* $427.00 of propane
(5 × $85.40) has been paid on that basis, never discussed. 8.2(c) already carves propane

**7. Reconciliation needs teeth.**

Lessor directing, instructing or disciplining Tiny Seed's employees. Given ongoing issues

recurring charge requires written notice and supporting documentation before it appears on
