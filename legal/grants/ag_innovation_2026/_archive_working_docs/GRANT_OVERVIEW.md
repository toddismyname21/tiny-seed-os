## Eight Priority Areas

1. Improve energy efficiency, water quality, reduce water consumption, reduce odors, address solid waste
2. **Improve production, processing, commercialization or utilization of ag commodities** ← PRIMARY FIT
3. Energy from agricultural sources (manure, food waste, biomass)
4. Low-carbon/no-carbon energy from ag commodities
5. **Technology to assist with farm management, including diagnostic tools** ← FITS (tracking system, Real Cost Accounting Tool)
6. **Technology for more efficient sorting, grading, or processing of ag commodities** ← FITS (post-harvest)
7. **Improve biosecurity or health of plants** ← FITS (sanitization, food safety)
8. Cybersecurity or data analytics

---

## Five Innovation Principles (Evaluation Criteria)

a) **Relevant** — addresses a real agricultural challenge
b) **Practical** — doesn't burden users or add complications
c) **Accessible** — can be used directly by farmers at farm level
d) **Scalable** — applicable to small/medium farms statewide
e) **Triple Bottom Line** — improves people, planet, profit

## Allowable Costs

### Equipment
- New equipment (innovative technology, specialized machinery, hardware)
- Used equipment (must be refurbished by OEM with warranty)
- Categories: Precision agriculture, Robotics/AI, Automation, Energy, Labor augmentation, Biosecurity
- Software **associated with equipment** (but NOT standalone software development)

### Supplies
- Materials needed to install or integrate the project

### Contractual (must be directly related to grant project)
- Consulting (project planning/coordination)
- Design (engineering or design fees)
- Labor (installation directly related to grant project)

**Important:** Infrastructure projects over $25,000 subject to prevailing wage

---

## Unallowable Costs

### Equipment
- Used equipment (without OEM refurb + warranty)
- Items that don't fit the 8 priority areas
- Non-specialized vehicles (cars, tractors, UTVs, skid-steers)
- Computers, tablets, phones
- Software development

### Personnel
- Wages/salary/stipends, travel, benefits

### Consumables
- Seeds, seedlings, plant matter, livestock, sampling
- Animal feed, supplements, medications
- Fertilizer, bio stimulants, soil amendments

### Other
- Purchases outside contract period
- Utility bills, taxes, liens, property, rent
- Lobbying/marketing/political contributions
- Administrative costs
- Materials not directly associated with project
- Other grant funds (cannot use as match)

---

## Excluded Activities
- Marijuana/cannabinoids
- Tobacco
- Alcohol processing (post-harvest)
- Software/App development
- Bench-scale/lab research only
- Projects not in PA
