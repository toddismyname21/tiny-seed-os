## Eligible Costs (What We Can Fund)

### Professional Services
- Accountants, CPAs, business consultants, financial planners
- Business planning and financial planning
- Profitability studies, feasibility analyses
- Financial statements (income, cash flow, balance sheets)
- Business software (e.g., QuickBooks) recommended by professionals
- Marketing strategies for new products/ventures
- Farm expansion planning
- New enterprise development (value-added processing, agritourism)
- Emergency preparedness planning

### Succession/Transition
- Succession planning with professional advisors
- Legal document preparation (entity formation, operating agreements)
- Farm appraisals related to succession
- Succession facilitators and mediators
- Transition of ownership plans (family or non-family)

### Diversification
- Business models and financial projections for new ventures
- Plans for on-farm value-added processing
- Agritourism planning
- Diversification of agricultural operations

---

## Ineligible Costs (Cannot Use Grant For)

- Land, buildings, equipment, facilities (capital purchases)
- Remodels, repairs, maintenance
- Conservation or nutrient management plans
- Surveying and subdivision costs
- Property taxes
- Insurance premiums
- Meals, mileage, travel expenses
- Costs already covered by other federal/state grants
- In-kind contributions (cannot count toward 25% match)

## Application Requirements

### Required Fields (Single Application System)
1. **Applicant Section** — business info, contact, NAICS code (use 8141 if individual)
2. **Project Overview** — project name
3. **Project Site** — location of farm operation
4. **Project Description** — detailed narrative of what the grant funds
5. **Program Objective Alignment** — which eligible purpose(s) the project serves
6. **Project Budget** — line items with costs
7. **Implementation Schedule** — timeline with milestones
8. **Participating Entities** — who will do the work (consultants, CPAs, etc.)
9. **Max Grant Amount Requested** — up to $15,000
10. **Attestation** — signed statement verifying accuracy

### To Get Reimbursed (After Project Completion)
- Project completion verification
- Completion date (within 2 years)
- Invoices and bills documenting actual expenses
- Any additional documentation PDA requests

---

## Application Steps

1. Create a Keystone Login account at grants.pa.gov
2. Log in to Single Application for Assistance system
3. Start new application
4. Complete all sections (can be done in any order, auto-saves)
5. Submit
6. PDA reviews in order received (first-come-first-served)
7. If approved: receive grant agreement, sign it
8. **Work should NOT begin until grant agreement is fully signed**
9. Complete project within 2 years
10. Submit invoices for reimbursement
