Common Categorization Mistakes

Cover crop seed: Could be Line 12 (conservation) OR Line 26 (seeds). If part of a conservation plan or purely for soil health, use Line 12. If it's part of your crop rotation plan, Line 26 is fine. Pick one approach and be consistent. Your accountant should decide.
Repair vs. Improvement: Fixing a broken greenhouse heater = Line 25 (repair). Replacing all greenhouse plastic with a better grade = Line 14 (depreciation/improvement). Rule of thumb: if it makes something better than it was, it's an improvement. If it puts it back to where it was, it's a repair.
Gas station purchases: Farm truck fuel = Line 10 or Line 19 depending on your method. Tractor diesel = Line 19. Snacks at the gas station = NOT deductible. Separate these at the register or split the transaction in QuickBooks.
Amazon / hardware store: These are the hardest to auto-categorize because you buy farm supplies AND personal items from the same vendors. Every Amazon and hardware store transaction needs manual review until you have enough training data.
Grant reimbursements: When PDA reimburses you for the Innovation Grant or Vitality Grant, that's income on Line 4a (agricultural program payments), NOT a reduction of the expense. You deduct the expense when you pay it AND report the reimbursement as income when you receive it.

Automation Training Plan

The goal: categorize manually now, build rules, then automate. Here's how the training data flows:

Step What You Do What Gets Trained
1. Manual
(Now)		 Categorize every transaction in QuickBooks using this map. Add a memo/note for ambiguous ones. Every categorized transaction becomes a training example: vendor + amount + description → Schedule F line
2. Rules
(After ~3 months)		 Review patterns. Create QuickBooks rules for vendors that always go to the same category (e.g., Johnny's = Seeds, OEFFA = Certification). Vendor-based rules handle ~60% of transactions automatically
3. Smart Rules
(After ~6 months)		 Build keyword-based rules for mixed vendors (e.g., Amazon purchase with "drip tape" in memo = Supplies). Keyword + vendor rules handle ~80% of transactions
4. Auto-Categorize
(Season 2+)		 Build auto-categorizer in Tiny Seed OS that suggests categories based on historical patterns. You approve or correct. ML model trained on your actual categorization history. Gets smarter with each correction.
Key principle: The manual work you do now IS the training data. Every transaction you categorize correctly teaches the system what the right answer is. Don't skip the manual phase — it's not wasted work, it's building the model.
