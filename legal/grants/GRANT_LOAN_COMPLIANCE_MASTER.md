## ⚠️ THE BIG PICTURE — three interlocked instruments
These are NOT three separate things. They are one financing structure:

```
FSA Operating Loan ($127,500)  →  fronts the cash to BUY the equipment
        ↓
Buy equipment (humidity control, compost turner, seeder, rock burier,
               harvester, harvest platform, cultivation system, delivery vehicle)
        ↓
AIG Grant ($75k + $37.5k match)  →  REIMBURSES those equipment purchases
        ↓
Grant reimbursement $$  →  MUST be PAID DOWN on the FSA operating loan
                            (explicit FSA loan condition)

FVPG Grant ($14,250)  →  separate track: reimburses BUSINESS-PLANNING costs
                          (Good Roots consulting), NOT equipment.
```
**If reimbursements aren't tracked and applied to the loan, you violate the FSA loan terms AND risk the grant.** This linkage is the #1 thing the system must enforce.

**⚠️ Borrower conditions you must not get blindsided by:**
- **FSA-2070 for EVERY piece of equipment purchased**, returned with the **vendor receipt**
- **Annual financial statements** to FSA, every year
- **No major capital purchases or sales without PRIOR FSA approval**
- **As grant funds are received → pay down the term operating loan** (the linkage above)
- **Financial management training REQUIRED** (FSA-2371) — complete within **2 years** with an approved vendor
- Report **any material change** in operation/income/expenses/security immediately
- Closing required **zero-balance payoffs** of: Shopify LOC, Shopify Credit, Amex 22002, PNC 8428, PNC 8003, PayPal Credit, Chase 7070, Ryan Utz, DGPerry, Kretschmann
- FSA-2040 Agreement for Use of Proceeds governs sales of secured crops/livestock/machinery
- Graduation clause: must move to conventional credit when available
